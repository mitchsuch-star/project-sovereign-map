Category guide:
- BUG: Code is wrong, crashes, or produces incorrect results
- BALANCE: Numbers are off, making something too strong/weak/irrelevant
- DESIGN: The system works as coded but the design itself is questionable
- ELEGANCE: Code works but is ugly, fragile, duplicated, or hard to maintain
- UX: Player-facing experience issue (confusing, frustrating, unclear)
- EXPLOIT: Player can abuse this for unintended advantage
- ARCHITECTURE: Structural issue with the codebase organization
