## §0 The word

The user, November 1, 2026, at RE-H's review: *"we have to make authority a usable currency as well, assure that gets hit, it should be a push and pull — mark that for next after events; other uses for auth, even if it's events."*

Read as three asks:
1. **A currency** — standing is something the Emperor *spends on purpose*, not only a figure that erodes when he answers his marshals badly.
2. **It gets hit** — the pulls land on the measured boards in every campaign, so a Decline priced at three standing is a real price and a letter's "100 → 97" is felt.

## §1 What authority is today — the survey (November 1, 2026; corrected November 3 by the gate's researchers)

### 1.1 The store
- **The player's:** `AuthorityTracker.authority`, 0–100, boots at 100 (`backend/models/authority.py`; built against the "always trust the marshals" exploit). `modify_authority(delta)` is clamped and carries no cause; `record_response(choice, turn)` records an objection answer (trust / insist / compromise). The threshold events at 70 ("Whispers of Weakness"), 50 ("Loss of Respect") and 30 ("Emperor in Name Only") fire **only from `record_response`** — an objection answered — and each once, ever (`_crossed_thresholds` is never re-armed in production); the client's box reads `title`/`message` while the event carries `name`/`description` (`main.gd:5353–5356`; `main.py:5553` copies it unchanged), so it prints "Authority Changed" with no text. No test pins it. (Filed at AU-1 as `BUG_FIXES.md` §AU's first rows.)
- **The grip:** `get_imperial_grip(world, nation)` = the authority (the player) or a flat 75 (every AI court) less 40 for a lost capital or 25 for a homeland minority, less 15 for a worst war score under −50 or 8 under −30. Derived, never stored, uncached (it already runs per marshal in combat and in the enemy AI). The aura `sovereign_aura_strength` is full at grip 85 (`AURA_GRIP_FULL`) and nothing at 30: the combat aura and the enemy's fear of it.
- **The courts':** `world.nation_authority[nation]`, boot 60 for every court (`nation_config.py`; the 1805 scenario authors none); `diplomacy.modify_nation_authority` has no production caller and `_process_nation_authority` is a documented placeholder; `reforms._spend_authority` writes both stores. **A court's standing only falls** — a political law costs 15 against `reforms.AI_AUTHORITY_FLOOR` 30, so a court makes two political acts and sits at 30 for ever: on the commanded saves Britain and Prussia read 30, Austria 30–45 and Russia 45 from turn 10 to turn 40. And `get_imperial_grip` never reads the table, so a court at 30 keeps a full aura, its vassal web never drifts and the Allies' courting never deepens on its account.

## §2 The problem, measured (re-stated November 3, 2026)

- **The peace is flat.** The hoarder sits at 100 for forty turns: no bill reaches him (the one letter priced in standing never comes at 100; his marshals' claims cost him nothing but their trust), and there is nothing to spend standing on but ten political laws.
- **The war is bled with no way back but a battle.** Every fighting board ends between 47 and 72, under the calm line (70), the defiance line (80) and the aura line (85), with no source but +2 / +5 wins and no purchase that would have made the fall a choice.
- **Nothing is spent on purpose.** The only deliberate spends are the laws at 15 and the letters' Declines (−3 / −5 / −8). The prices that exist are quoted (the laws, the letters) but the dents that land are not told: the vindication's −5, the trust pattern's −2 / −3 and the field's ±5 never show a figure or a cause, and the threshold boxes print no text.

So "neither spent nor felt" is true of the peace and "spent on nothing, bled by war" of the campaign. The gate's design gives the peace its bills, the war its way back, every player a menu, and every move its cause.

## §3 The brief (as queued November 1, 2026; answered by §4–§5)

- **Pushes** — acts the Emperor takes by his standing alone: overruling a marshal, a decree where a law costs gold, a court's or a client's compliance by prestige, a marshal's honour by the Emperor's word, a third button on a letter.
- **Pulls** — the events' Declines as today, plus consequences the player chose: a promise broken, a battle lost that he insisted on, a lie caught out, a remonstrance, a long absence from Paris. The aura line 85 crossed by design in every campaign.
- **Regrowth** — victories and treaties, the Temple, the coronation-class moments, so standing moves in a band and comes back by play, never by waiting alone.
- **The display** — the figure where the price is quoted and where it moves; the dent shown the morning it lands.
- **Building Blocks** — the AI courts' authority takes the same sinks and sources at their own values; no France-literal in the engine.
