# VP-R1 "The Road to Forty-Five" — the three probes before any code

> **September 25, 2026 · row EP follow-on, slice VP-R1 (`DESIGN_REFINEMENT.md` GEV-D1) · memo of record for STEP 0.** Every figure below was measured on master at `08ed3d37` (the GE-V commit) before a line of production code was written. A probe that contradicts the brief overrides the brief, and two of the three do. The probe scripts (`p1_muster_probe.py`, `p2_shutout_probe.py`, `p3_autonomous_probe.py` + `chain.py`) ran from the session scratchpad on the committed engine; their staging material is the GE-V session's own chunk saves (the boards at the end of each `gev-played-*` chunk) plus the shipped 1805 boot and the committed Pressburg fixture. The build that follows, its levers and the re-measure are recorded in §4 and §5 below, appended after the build.

## 0. The verdicts in three lines

| Probe | The brief's premise | Measured | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 the muster** | "the preview overstates arrival by about a third" | the committed figure ("X if all march") is an honest EXPECTATION — within 20% of the Monte-Carlo mean on **74 of 74** supported strikes, preview ÷ mean median **0.994**; a single draw lands within 20% of it on **61 of 74** (82%) | **the fix is the PREVIEW, not the rule** — the number is right and its LABEL and its ROWS lie: "if all march" reads as a promise and is neither the ceiling nor the mean, and **35 of 100 strikes print a "WILL JOIN" row for a corps the same arithmetic prices at 0%** |
| **P2 the shut-out** | "can France close 16 of 26 without a war on Britain's friends?" | **No.** Boot 10 (38%); the Pressburg shape minus the fixture's gifts **11 (42%)**; + a beaten Austria's forced-alliance clause 12; + every Congress signatory in the System (a mechanic that does not exist) 15 (58%); 16 needs a war on the Pope or on Denmark | **60% is unreachable from play; the line moves to 50% = 13 BEFORE the reach work** |
| **P3 the autonomous charge** | "count glory attacks fired at unfavorable odds" | three fired across the ambient board and both hand-played openings; **two of the three at `unfavorable`** — ratio **0.198** (Murat → John at fortified Tyrol under Charles's 52k, −9,891) and **0.279** (Murat → John at Franconia, −2,127); the third at `even` (0.83, won) | **a defect, not design** — the autonomous attack obeys no odds gate at all, while a delegation-inferred attack stops at 0.7; both boards get the gate |

## 1. P1 — the muster (the preview vs the field)

**Method.** 117 supported-strike candidates enumerated over 14 staged boards (the boot + the thirteen chunk saves of the two GE-V openings): every standing French lead × every at-war enemy in reach. Each strike was driven through the real `CommandExecutor.execute` on a fresh copy of the board with `_muster_confirmed` set (a cautious objection answered INSIST), once on the driver's own per-turn seed (the "canonical" draw, which reproduces the archived arrivals — Ney's turn-1 strike on Mack arrived with Davout, Lannes and Napoleon here as in the digest) and forty more times on independent seeds. Per strike the table holds the preview's `committed_strength` and `ceiling_strength`, the realized committed strength (the lead plus every arrival's `_committed_share` on pre-battle strengths — the resolver's own term), the Monte-Carlo mean and P10/P50/P90, and for every candidate his personality, relationship to the lead, departing terrain, deterministic arrival score, threshold, P(arrive), P(arrive under a written SUPPORT order) and Monte-Carlo arrival rate. 100 strikes resolved, 17 refused honestly (recovering corps, cornered, engaged).

**Measured.**

| Figure | Value |
|---|---|
| supported strikes with a preview | 74 |
| preview committed within 20% of the Monte-Carlo mean | **74 / 74** |
| preview ÷ Monte-Carlo mean (median · mean) | 0.994 · 0.999 |
| canonical single draw within 20% of the preview | 61 / 74 (82%) |
| mean per-strike fraction of draws within 20% | 0.768 |
| over-promised > 20% on the canonical draw · under | 6 · 7 |
| expected reinforcement as a share of the ceiling's reinforcement (mean, 71 strikes) | **0.66** |
| adjacent "WILL JOIN" candidates | 248 |
| … priced at P(arrive) = 0 | **46** (all 46 depart from MOUNTAINS — Munich or Tyrol; 44 aggressive Ney/Murat at logistics 3–4, 2 cautious) |
| … at 0 < P < 0.5 | 48 |
| strikes carrying at least one "WILL JOIN" row priced at 0% | **35 of 100** |
| mean P(arrive) by name | Ney 0.23 · Murat 0.26 · Davout 0.43 · Lannes 0.82 · Napoleon 0.91 · Massena 0.96 |
| mean P(arrive) by departing terrain | plains 0.84 · mountains 0.51 |
| of the 46 zero-odds rows, P(arrive) under a written SUPPORT order | 0.77 ×15 · 0.47 ×11 · 0.18 ×14 · still 0 ×6 |

**The memo's own example, re-read.** Soult's turn-8 strike at Franconia (GE-V §2.1): *"21,691; 38,878 if all march, up to 61,779"* — measured here on the same board: preview 38,878, canonical realized **40,262**, Monte-Carlo mean 38,991, P10/P90 26,947/51,657. The preview did not overstate by a third; **the memo compared the CEILING (61,779) to the field**, because the label "if all march" reads as "everyone comes" while the number under it is the arrival-weighted mean (PT-A2, September 2026) and the true everyone-comes figure is the "up to" clause. What the memo saw as a lottery the preview overstates is a lottery the preview prices correctly and labels wrongly.

**The systematic no-show is real, but it is the ROW, not the roll.** The arrival formula (MULTI_MARSHAL_SPEC §7, design) reads the DEPARTING province's terrain: mountains −20. "The whole body to Munich" (opening B) stood the army on a mountain province, and from there Ney (aggressive, logistics 4) scores 55 against a 65 threshold with ±8 jitter — **zero in seventeen** — and Murat 50; Davout (logistics 7) 60, one in six. The preview's ARITHMETIC already prices all three at their true odds (which is why the number is honest), but their ROWS read "WILL JOIN — will march to the sound of the guns". Forty of the forty-six zero-odds corps become reachable under a written SUPPORT order (+10 to the score, threshold 60), the lever the game already has — and the preview names that lever only for the literal and for Bernadotte, never for a man it has just priced at nothing.

**Decision (P1): the PREVIEW.** (i) The headline names the figure as what it is — *"expect about 38,878 with the corps likely to arrive"* — and keeps "if all march" as the CEILING's label, in the position the brief asked for. (ii) Every WILL JOIN row carries its arrival odds in words and, at zero, says so and names the lever: *"— from the mountains at Munich he will not make it in time (0 in 6); order 'Ney, support Soult' and he likely would (3 in 4)"*. No rule moves: the arithmetic is honest, `_calculate_reinforcements` is untouched, M1/M1b/M4 read `_committed_reinforcement_strength` with `world=None` and cannot move, and `BASELINE_SERIES` cannot move for a display change. GEV-D1's second completion item ("within 20% of the arrival on 8 of 10 driven strikes") is **met by the existing arithmetic** on the canonical draw (61 of 74 = 8.2 of 10) and pinned as such; what the slice adds is that the player can now read the same thing.

## 2. P2 — the shut-out reach (16 of 26 is not on any road)

**Method.** `naval.closure_against(world, "Britain")` and `congress._shut_out_reading` run on the boot, on the committed Pressburg fixture (`fixture_ge3_pressburg.json`, turn 28) as is, on the fixture MINUS its gifts (Naples and Portugal restored to their crowns; Denmark, Sweden and Russia at PEACE with Britain — the boot's own truth), and on four arms a played road can add without a war on Britain's friends (Portugal 40 and Naples 30 are her hosts; Sweden her 1805 ally).

| Board | Closed / 26 | % | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| the boot | 10 (France 4, Spain 3, Holland 2, Kingdom of Italy 1) | 38.5 | 0 |
| the Pressburg fixture as committed | 20 (+ Denmark 2, Sweden 2, Russia 2 at war with Britain; Naples 1, Lisbon 2, Hanover 1 held) | 76.9 | 2 |
| **the Pressburg shape minus the gifts** | **11** (+ Hanover held) | **42.3** | 1 |
| + Austria in the System (a forced-alliance clause at the peace table) | 12 | 46.2 | 1 |
| + Prussia and Russia in the System too ("every Congress signatory closes her ports" — **a mechanic that does not exist**; only a forced-alliance clause or a satellite's lord puts a court in the System) | 15 | 57.7 | 1 |
| + Rome held (a war on the Papal States) | 16 | 61.5 | 2 |
| + Denmark at war with Britain (Copenhagen) | 18 | 69.2 | 2 |

The France-vs-Britain war score reads **0** on every board including the fixture's turn 28: the SUES arm (war score ≤ −40 or a capital taken) has no producer on a Continental campaign — Britain's soil is unreachable and her landings are the only battles — so "a naval war-score sue arm for Britain" would be a new component, not a number.

**Decision (P2): `cs_shutout_pct` 60 → 50 (13 of 26), moved BEFORE the reach work,** at both homes (`congress.CS_SHUTOUT_PCT`, `europe_1805.json campaign_end`), the MODDING_FORMAT default and the four pins that read sixteen. Thirteen is the smallest whole number above half, and it is reached from the Pressburg shape by a beaten Austria's forced-alliance clause plus ONE walk-in (Rome) or ONE neutral war (Denmark), or — if the Congress signatories' ports ever close by treaty — with no neutral war at all. **Recorded as the recommended design if the re-measure still finds SHUT OUT unreached: a court that RECOGNIZES the Congress by treaty closes its ports to the trade-dominance court for the sitting** (the Tilsit reading; 15 of 26 from the Pressburg shape alone). Not built here — it is a mechanic beyond the brief's three options and the 50 line is enough on the measured boards.

## 3. P3 — the autonomous charge (two of three below the floor)

**Method.** The real driver, wrapped: before `jealousy.process_autonomous_attacks` fires, every warned aggressive marshal's target is read off `find_autonomous_attack_target` and priced through the muster's own arithmetic (`_muster_reason` → `_committed_reinforcement_strength(expected_at)` for our side, `_defender_muster` for theirs, `inferred_attack_odds_band` on both) — the band the preview would print if the player had ordered the same attack; the enemy-side P3.9 lookups are recorded the same way. Replayed: the 40-turn ambient board; opening B's six chunks and opening A's seven, from the boot, on their archived dials. **Both replays reproduce the archive** (Murat −9,891 on turn 3, "Murat stood alone"; titled 37→35 by turn 12 on B, 38→41→35 on A).

**Decision (P3): the gate, both boards.** The player-side processor and the AI's P3.9 rung read ONE predicate before the attack goes in — the same band and the same 0.7 floor a delegation-inferred attack has obeyed since CR-5 — and at `unfavorable` the attack is refused with the WO-28 refusal beat naming the odds (*"… but the odds held him — 1 to 5 against Charles's guns"*); the grievance persists, the standing order is restored, nothing else changes. "Fires WITH the muster" was already true (the resolver rolls his reinforcements; they were priced at zero from the mountains), so the gate is the half that was missing.
