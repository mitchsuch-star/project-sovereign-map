# WO-EVAL — "How do we make the game better?" · August 17, 2026

**Brief:** the ten-campaign weird-outcomes playtest
(`docs/audits/PLAYTEST_WEIRD_OUTCOMES_2026_08_16.md`) put one question to this
session: *the only strategy this game truly implements is attack — which of the
four non-military strategies do we commit to making real, and what does the
battle become if it stops being free?*

**Status: this memo is the GATE RECORD.** §5 is the plan, §6 is what the user
must answer, §7 is what must not be silently reversed. **Report-only: zero
production code touched.**

**Method.** Eight parallel investigations against master `24a59ed`, each
*required to reproduce before recommending*; every one then handed to an
adversarial verifier whose default position was that it is wrong; then a
completeness critic running independent replications. ~40 probe scripts.
**Two investigations were overturned outright (WO-D2, WO-D5), five materially
corrected, one survived intact (WO-D6's mechanism).** Claims marked
**[hand-verified]** were additionally reproduced by the session author directly,
not delegated.

---

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

**The most valuable output of this evaluation is a correction to the evidence
base it was convened on: the funnel's magnitude is not established, because the
instrument that measured it is nondeterministic.** `tools/playtest_driver.py`
sets `SOVEREIGN_SEED` (the AI campaign seed) but never seeds the module RNG —
`grep -c random tools/playtest_driver.py` = **0**, `grep -rn "random.seed"
backend/` = **nothing**, while **20 backend modules** import `random` for
combat, arrival rolls, defiance, jealousy and vassal outcomes. `BASELINE_SERIES`
is reproducible only because *its own* runner re-seeds per turn and pins
`PYTHONHASHSEED=0` (`tests/test_ai_intent_threat_migration.py:532`, `:777`); the
driver does neither. **[hand-verified]** Three byte-identical invocations of the
committed `weird_tyrant.json` at its committed seed end at **30 / 28 / 27**
provinces and diverge on turn 1 (treasury 2693 / 2689 / 2535). The fleet's
longer re-runs spread further: Tyrant **4 · 28 · 17 · 16 · 14**, Kingmaker
(recorded as *annihilated at 5*) **28 · 28 · 12**. The bands overlap completely.
Compounding it, `tools/playtest_runs/` is gitignored (`.gitignore:89`; `git
ls-files` returns 0) **[hand-verified]**, so every digest the memo cites is a
local artifact that the next run overwrites — and the driver was answering
roughly a third of the game's surfaces with silence (`"state"`, `envoy_digest`,
`jealousy_attacks` — **0 occurrences each** **[hand-verified]**). **What
survives, and survives strongly, is the mechanism work**, all of it static and
all of it reproduced by a hostile verifier: the muster's monotone casualty
curve, the depot's dominance arithmetic, `power_score`'s blindness to wealth,
the garrison that cannot fall, the dispatch's class collision, the dead
AI-vs-AI peace routes, and the four P1s. **So: fix the instrument first (one
session, zero production code), re-run the arms with error bars, and hold the
two structural gate calls until there is a funnel that has one.** Everything
else in §5 proceeds in parallel and depends on none of it.

## 2. The funnel: is it real?

**Verdict: the funnel's *shape* is probably real. Its *magnitude* is unmeasured,
and the sentence in `STATUS.md` — "every arm that fought ended at 29–31; every
arm that tried statecraft, clients, commerce or the sea ended at 5–12" — cannot
currently be defended.**

Same committed scripts, same `"seed": "historical"`, same policy, `--fresh`:

| arm | memo | independent re-runs |
|---|---|---|
| **Tyrant** (fights; memo *"won"*) | 29 | **30 · 28 · 27** (14t, hand) · **4 · 28 · 17 · 16 · 14** (30t, fleet) |
| **Merchant** (never fights; *"survived untouched"*) | 28 | **27 · 27 · 20 · 13** |
| **Kingmaker** (statecraft; *"annihilated"*) | 5 | **28 · 28 · 12** |

A fighting arm at 4 and a statecraft arm at 28 twice is a same-input spread that
exceeds the claimed between-arm effect. The trajectories are real campaigns, not
parse artifacts (`28 28 28 … 26 25 25 24 … 18 16 16`).

**Why it was missable, and was missed three times.** Three investigations hit
the nondeterminism independently and each scoped it locally as a caveat on its
own row. `docs/PLAYTESTING.md`'s *Known-bad digests* section does not mention
run-to-run variance at all.

**The second instrument defect points the same way.** The driver is
structurally blind to the statecraft inbox: `envoy_digest` is truthy on **134 of
202** responses across four weird arms and is read nowhere. Measured over 14
turns, the **Kingmaker** arm was offered **22 letters and answered 0**; the
**Merchant** **23 and answered 0**. Those are two of the four data points behind
the funnel. (Honest qualifier: those are IGR-F *routine* minor-court asks whose
default outcome is to lapse, close to the driver's stated `decline` policy.
Twenty-two routine letters do not explain a 5-province annihilation. This
weakens the evidence; it does not overturn the shape.) Add `_option_id`
blindness — the World Burns arm's **fifteen declare-war ceremonies declared war
on zero nations** (A/B: `0 → 8` after a one-line widening) — and the `battles`
counter under-reading by 2.5×–∞.

**A posture confound the memo did not control for. [hand-verified]** Reading the
committed scripts: the Admiral arm ordered its entire army `hold position` for
30 turns and issued **0** of 54 commands with any military content; the
Kingmaker opened a **fourth** war (Papal States) while already fighting three
great powers and dispersed Massena to Rome. Meanwhile the **Merchant** fortified
all seven marshals and the **Pacifist** sued for peace — and those two
non-military arms are the ones that held 28–29 provinces. So even at face value
the memo's own table contains two counterexamples to its headline: **two arms
that never attacked once kept the map.**

**The right framing.** Not *"attack is the only implemented verb."* Every
non-military system in this docket was found **built and working** when driven by
hand: `propose_vassal` is a live priced proposal that creates clients; the naval
Descent runs end to end to a London garrison assault in ~13 turns for ~5,200g;
peace with Russia scores **ACCEPT** on every measured turn from t16. The accurate
sentence is:

> **The non-military strategies are built, unpriced, unreachable and invisible —
> in that order of severity — and the harness that measured them could not press
> most of their buttons.**

That reframing changes the gate. The question is no longer *"which of four
systems do we commit to building?"* — three of the four are built. It is *"what
does wealth or a client buy that the map does not already give you, and what is
the honest price of the free lunches that exist today?"* That is one question
(§6 G1 and G2), not four.

## 3. Row by row

### WO-D1 — should the battle be a decision?

**Premise verdict: PARTLY WRONG in four places, two load-bearing.**

- **The 1:13.9 exchange ratio is a harness artifact.**
  `casualty_summary.attacker_casualties` is the **lead corps only** and the code
  says so (`attacker_casualties_scope`, `combat_executor.py:1877`
  **[hand-verified]**); `playtest_driver.py:314` reads it anyway. On the
  canonical turn-1 `Ney, attack Mack` the player's own terminal already prints
  `Ney's army 2,485, Mack 14,785` (1:5.95) while the digest field says 764
  (1:19.35). Whole-side over four arms: **1:4.31** attacking, **1:0.95**
  defending. The memo's own 1:13.9 also fails to re-derive from its digests
  (1:11.33).
- **Docket lever (b) — symmetric defender concentration — is ALREADY SHIPPED.**
  `_calculate_reinforcements` is called for *both* sides
  (`combat_executor.py:5000`/`:5003` **[hand-verified]**), `committed_defender`
  landed as CA9-F1. Measured firing: Massena 42,000 attacking John 20,000 at
  Tyrol → **defender committed 63,028**, France routed 8/8 seeds. **Do not build
  it.** The real asymmetry is that the *attacker chooses the battle*, which is
  correct design.
- **Docket lever (a) — capping auto-reinforcement — is measurably
  counter-productive.** A 2-corps cap still wins 96% of the time, and 2 corps at
  Swabia pay **0%** supply attrition against **11,340 men/turn** for the full
  muster. Supply is **68% of France's measured losses** (weird_tyrant, 25 turns:
  4,050 attacking + 22,256 defending + **56,120 to supply**). Capping deletes
  the game's dominant cost of war; France comes out *stronger*.
- **PC15-D2 already ruled on this five weeks ago:** *"the concentration tax is
  DESIGN and stands; what changed is that the remedy is named."*

**The genuine gap — two items, neither of which is "the battle is not a
decision":**

1. **The muster preview never states its price.** `_build_muster_preview`
   (`combat_executor.py:959`) emits rows, odds, presence and a hint — and no
   supply term, though `world.get_effective_supply_cap` is one call away. The
   screen the player commits from promises *"78,676 if all march"* and never
   says Swabia feeds 60,000.
2. **The attacker's casualty base is the lead's own strength**
   (`combat.py:268`) while effective strength credits the whole muster
   (`:1126`). Measured over 300 seeds/point: **adding 165,000 men cuts your own
   dead by 79%** (6,592 → 1,417). That is the mechanic that does not exist.
3. Found by the verifier, and it decides whether the docket's own third lever is
   even buildable: `_get_casualty_participants` (`:1717`) filters on
   region/nation/strength/broken/retreated — **not** on `fortified` or
   `holding_position`. **A corps already standing on the battle province commits
   its weight and bleeds no matter what order it holds.** The game has no order
   that excludes a co-located corps. → gate **G3**.

| option | shape | cost | blast radius |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — "The Muster States Its Price"** | the preview quotes the supply arithmetic; ledger/panel/preview single-sourced onto `get_effective_supply_cap`; the free HOLD/fortify exclusion clause on *adjacent* rows; harness reads the whole-army figure | **1 session** | M1–M7 no · BASELINE_SERIES no (preview is player-gated at `:4808`) · one `.gd` branch · 0 fields |
| **2 — "The Army Bleeds, Not The Lead"** | casualty base = headcount present, symmetric, **plus** the `×1.5` victor predicate re-decided in the same slice | 2–3 sessions | BASELINE_SERIES **moves** · 9 test files |
| **3 — "Name the March"** | exclusion set on the muster confirm | 1.5–2 | re-opens the CA9-D2 verbatim user ruling; machinery absent for the co-located case |
| **4 — cap the march** | — | 0.5 | **rejected, measured backwards** |

**Recommendation: Option 1. Hold Option 2 behind one played campaign with its
exit condition written now.** The re-cost matters: `ledger.py:187`/`:202` and
`world_state.py:8361` ship the **raw** `supply_capacity` (Swabia 40,000) while
the bill uses the **effective** cap (60,000 on allied soil), rendered as
`Supply: 40,000` by `region_panel.gd:195`. Printing the honest 60,000 in the
preview while the panel says 40,000 re-creates the PT-D5 two-figures-one-label
trap this slice exists to remove — single-source them together. Route as an
**amendment to PC15-D2's own deferred region-panel row**, not a new finding.

**The argument against, honestly.** A player who reads the honest numbers may
shrug and mass anyway; Option 1 changes nothing mechanically. If the objection is
*"189,000 men losing 1,417 is not a battle,"* only Option 2 answers it — and
Option 2 alone converts the 2–4-corps band from 96–100% attacker victory to
**0–20% win / 97.7–100% stalemate**, which additionally fires the
reinforcer-withdrawal arm at `:6256`, dissolving the doomstack and deleting the
recurring supply bill it is meant to complement. A worse game, reached in three
sessions.

**Premise verdict: WRONG. The investigation that examined it was overturned;

**And the real design question neither the docket nor the investigation

That is not a feature needing a shop window — it is an **unpriced backdoor
beside a working priced front door**. → gate **G1**.

**Recommendation: ship A′ (the four items, 0.5 session) + D (the WO-8 per-target
courting cap, 0.5 session) now; take the pricing question to the gate.**

WO-8 is the soundest item in the docket and fires **unprompted**: a 30-turn

**The argument against.** A′ + D makes the layer *honest*; it does not make it a

orders at all. The layer's output is army and gold; the funnel is scored in
provinces; nothing teaches the conversion. Only a per-turn client levy changes
that, and its payoff currency has nothing to buy until Victory & Objectives
lands.

### WO-D4 — what is the reward for not fighting?

**Premise verdict: PARTLY WRONG on the numbers, RIGHT and important on the
structure.**

- **Market vs depot** **[hand-verified arithmetic]**: depot 300g / +50 flat
  income / +10,000 supply; market 350g / +25% of base
  (`region.py:100-104`, `:389-393`). Market only wins when base income > 200. The
  memo's *"10 of 13 buildable provinces"* confuses slots with provinces: France
  has **12 buildable provinces and 13 slots**. Depot **strictly** dominates on
  **7**, **weakly** on 4, and the market wins at Paris where both get built. So
  **11 of 12**.
- **The Bavaria 3→11 evidence is dead** — Bavaria peaks at 6 on t2 and is back
  to its boot 3 by t4 in every re-run.
- **The structural finding reproduced to the digit and is the row's real
  content:**

```
BOOT                             power_score=84  share=0.396  gold=800     force_limit=130000
FULL 13-SLOT BUILD-OUT + 100,000 GOLD
                                 power_score=84  share=0.396  gold=100000  force_limit=130000
ONE PROVINCE TAKEN, 0 GOLD       power_score=87  share=0.400  gold=0       force_limit=132500
ARMY DOUBLED (378,000)           power_score=84  share=0.396
```

`coalition.power_score` (`coalition.py:148`) is `region_count × tier_weight`,
full stop — and it is the single source behind hegemony pressure, coalition
formation, the agenda hegemon, D3's eclipse gate and the Balance of Europe tab.
The AI's assessment of a foreign court reads exactly **one** economic fact:
`nation_gold < 0` (`intent.py:349`). **Europe can see that you are bankrupt; it
cannot see that you are rich.** France is 17.8% of Europe by income and 29.7% by
power score.

Two sub-claims were killed and both narrow the row: `get_force_limit` is a
**price, not a gate** (`economy_executor.py:417-423`; France boots 45% over and
simply pays), and the levy is **open** — the Merchant arm's infantry pool ran
80,000 → 100,000 **capped and unused** while the treasury climbed. So gold→army
is open and gold→a court's behaviour is open. **The only closed conversion is
gold→standing.**

**Recommendation: ship A ("The Slot States Its Terms" — build chips quote
cost/yield/upkeep/supply live, 0.5–1 session, but the seam list must include
`get_filtered_game_state_summary` (`world_state.py:8425`) and the `-1` fog
sentinel or it ships production-dead) and D (the written requirement handed to
Victory). Do not re-price depot/market — four variants all merely invert the
winner to 12/12 market-preferred. Take the standing question to the gate (G2).**

D's content, because it is the row's (b) deliverable: **exposed today, GR5, no
new code** — `calculate_turn_income` with its full signed component set,
`calculate_turn_upkeep`, `nation_gold`, `get_force_limit`,
`ledger._build_economy`, naval trade/closure, `campaign_ledgers`, war exhaustion,
vassal loyalty, agenda satisfaction; reading all of it for all 20 nations costs
**2.8 ms**, so a per-turn victory reader is GR8-affordable. **Missing and
load-bearing:** (1) an economic *share* — nothing computes one, and the raw
figures show why it matters (the Ottoman Empire is the **second-largest economy
in Europe at 10.5%**); (2) a *stock* that rises (every economic quantity is a
flow, and EB-1 makes the one stock self-limiting); (3) an **opposition hook** —
the share must feed at least one AI reading or an economic victory is a
scoreboard, not a game. The development axis already has an owner: **ES-4,
`ECONOMY_REVISIT_SPEC.md:386`, EC-2 pass 2.**

**The argument against A.** A player who reads the honest numbers will correctly
conclude that building is not worth doing: the entire 13-slot lifetime build-out
pays **+347 net/turn for 4,150g**, against **+1,125/turn plus +17,500 force limit
plus 8 more slots** for conquering Austria's seven provinces. There is a serious
case for skipping to G2. My reason for not leading with it: 100,000 gold moves
France's standing by **exactly 0.000** and one province moves it — that is a
change of *category*, not of tuning, and it is the user's to make.
