## 8. The scored creative pass

Scored against the two pillars §4.7 names, from the sweep's own
artifacts (dispatch streams, campaign logs, script logs) — not from
memory of the code.

**Do the majors read as distinct statesmen? 8.0.** In one ambient
digest: Britain pays Austria's war bills 23 times, commissions Paget
and Shrapnel, and recruits on ten turns — the paymaster with a deep
bench. Russia sits at the arbiter posture, funds Sardinia against
Austria late, and (scripted) signs the Tilsit reversal the moment
generosity makes it thinkable. Austria fights everyone, recruits on 13
turns, and authors three revanche grievances in other courts' decks —
the sweep's true aggressor. Prussia hovers at `align 59`, passes
through `bandwagon` when its prize's holder stumbles, and dies for it
by turn 9 of the passive world. The wartime homogeneity guard
(pairwise-distinct behavioural histograms) is now a standing pin, not a
vibe. What keeps this from 9: the ambient world's drama is
Austria-shaped on most seeds — the variance moves timings and rosters
more than it moves the protagonist.

**Does the player have something to do about them? 8.0.** The scripted
arm is the demonstration: a crisis fore-warned on the dispatch, priced
in gold, bought off for 1 332g, and RECEIPTED with its cause named; a
guarantee that measurably deters (run 1 deterred the crisis by
accident); a renege that turns Europe's reading of France 82→97
overnight; a beaten great power courted into switching sides and aimed
at Finland. Every instrument runs through an honest executor gate that
refuses nonsense with a reason in character ("one does not bankroll
the court one is fighting"). The courting stream (40 asks in 40 passive
turns) keeps the participation surface alive even for a player who
never acts. What keeps this from 9: scenes 1 and 7's player-visible
halves await a played world, and the volte staging showed how narrow
the receptivity window is (the R49 shed) — a real player will need the
Talleyrand counsel to find it.

**The living balance (wars start and end without France): 6.5.** The
honest weak edge, unchanged in character since AI-3r: pair-level peaces
fire on every seed and both courts bleed into them, but no ambient
council war and no standalone third-party settlement occurred in 400
acceptance turns. The predicate is written, the chain is
deterministically reachable, and the seam channel shows spontaneous
AI-vs-AI violence exists — but the marquee "two rivals exhaust
themselves while France rearms" remains a played-moment event, not an
ambient certainty. This is the pillar to re-measure at the first
long human campaign.

**Legibility of the machinery: 8.5.** Everything that happened left a
named record: the receipt names its cause, the war carries its stated
reason, the promotion names its author and its grievance, the tail
collapses the overflow grammatically, and the narration cap held at 2
on every turn of every run while the beats stayed structurally exempt.

**Stage G verdict:** the phase's systems are alive, discriminable,
