# Marshal Content Pass — Gate Recommendations (July 10, 2026)

> **Status:** PROPOSAL — awaiting the Marshal Content Pass USER DESIGN GATE. Nothing herein is approved or implemented. Produced July 10, 2026 by a three-lens design panel (20 agents: seam/cost grounding, history/mechanics/drama designers + skills-relationships author, per-marshal adversarial verification, MC-4 judge, completeness critique, synthesis).

**What this is:** the MC-1..MC-4 design package for `docs/MARSHAL_CONTENT_PASS_SPEC.md`, assembled by a three-lens design panel (historical fidelity / systems mechanics / drama), code-verified seam by seam, then adversarially critiqued for completeness. All contradictions the critique found have been reconciled below; **the verdicts in this memo are authoritative** — where earlier panel notes disagreed (a Lannes morale dampener, a Charles ×0.7 morale multiplier, a Murat +6k figure), those were rejected variants and do not appear here. Nothing gets built until you bless this. MC-0 (ability display bug) already landed July 4.

Context you should hold while reading: the July-10 creative audit named **roster content as the binding constraint on fun**, and the design budget is **~10–12 wired abilities across 21 marshals**, one per marshal max, decisions-not-stat-sticks, minors stay personality-only.

### Design notes on the non-obvious rows

**Ney — the shock-9 reconciliation.** The panel's balance math was computed at scenario-default shock 5 (+8% relative); the MC-2 table below gives Ney shock 9, which changes the ability to 1.45→1.55 ≈ +7% relative on a substantially stronger base. Both are under the ~+10% anchor. Sequencing resolves the tension: **MC-1 lands the ability at current defaults; MC-2's skills landing re-measures and records the +7% figure.** The ability's relative value is stable either way; the earlier "no skills dict for Ney" instruction is superseded by the MC-2 table. One wiring trap all lenses missed: the dispatch gates on `trigger` before name, so the JSON must carry `"trigger": "when_attacking"` or the ability is silently dead.

**Davout — why the set's only T2.** The archive (`SPECIAL_ABILITIES_EVALUATION.md` Part 1) already adjudicated this space and recommended Iron Resolve over the T0 Counter-Punch re-key (reactive; dead if the enemy avoids him). The critical correction the panel added against code: **a fortified marshal cannot attack** (`executor.py:776-784`), so stacks MUST survive the unfortify action or the ability is unusable — pinned by test. The release always costs 1 AP plus the fortify defense bonus, so peak +24% never stacks on a fortified posture: stronger self-balancing than proposed. Turtling is capped (3 stacks, fortify decay untouched); any attack consumes all stacks, so no banking.

**Soult — corrected numbers, and the MC-4 dependency.** Two factual errors were fixed against code: drill's payoff is **+20%** attack (`marshal.py:902`), not +10%, and drilling in enemy contact is impossible (`tactical_executor.py:209-231` blocks it) — so "drill under fire" was struck. Amortized value is ~+10%/attack (Ney's anchor tier) paid in tempo, AP, and one enemy phase at −25% defense. This ability is also **MC-4's centerpiece**: it converts the CR-5 literal recast into character (see §5). If the gate cuts this row, MC-4's canonization loses its payload — decide them together.

**Lannes — what was rejected.** The panel's strongest-rejected alternative was a per-marshal morale-loss dampener: killed because the coordinated battle path applies **uniform** morale deltas to all participants (`combat_executor.py:3571/3580`) — a per-marshal exception is a structural change to the just-landed W6-11 blessed morale curve (escalation-class), and it's invisible. The +15 arrival design is visible every time it fires and deliberately mirrors Bernadotte's −15: **the pair is the drama centerpiece — 90 vs 60 deterministic base — and should be blessed jointly.** A terrain-penalty waiver variant was also rejected (over-tuned; deletes failure drama).

**Murat — 5k, not 6k, and lead-from-the-front only.** No anchor supports a 6k premium; 5k is the exact balance-audited dormant Pursuit Master constant, and the cavalry flag already makes it exclusive. The coordinated path grants pursuit only to the **primary attacker** — Murat must lead the killing blow; arriving as a reinforcement earns nothing (pinned). Recklessness escalation is deliberately untouched: win-streaking Murat still walks into the 2×-both-ways glorious-charge trap. W6-11 makes routs more common than when 5k was audited — if playtest shows over-annihilation, tune to 4k in-band, never add conditions.

**Massena — the guardrail, not the recast.** His aggressive personality stays (the reverted-recast lesson); the ability is the guardrail that makes his cornered Italian theater survivable. Rejected: +15% (3× the always-on anchor) and a rout-threshold-12 variant (invisible, and incoherent with the garrison morale-floor clamps). +10% exactly matches the cautious outnumbered grant he can't have (he's aggressive) — the precedented ceiling for one defensive trick. The shave-your-army-to-stay-outnumbered edge is a strictly losing trade (linear strength for a 10% multiplier).

**Bernadotte — the negative ability (explicit blessing required, Q1).** Defiance alone would be a dead slot (it fires only after MODERATE+ objection + insistence, rare for a cautious 17k corps); the arrival arm makes "Bernadotte does not march" a recurring, visible event — and the truer history. The counter-lever is built in: put the order in writing (SUPPORT, +10) and he mostly comes, at the cost of AP and micromanagement, which IS the fantasy. His MC-2 trust is **40** (the roster floor — earlier drafts said 55; 40 is authoritative and keeps the French trust mean at exactly 70.0). Trust 40 is defiance-inert (the modifier bites at ≤20/≥80) — it's Jealousy/MC-3 substrate, not a defiance lever.

**Charles — the hybrid, and what was cut.** The ×0.7 morale-loss dampener was rejected (compounds to ~2× rout endurance, invisible, and requires fragile per-participant surgery on the W6-11 uniform-delta path). What ships: the free T0 activation of the already-wired +3%, plus the legible rout-threshold extension wired at **both** forced-retreat copies (missing the coordinated copy would make it vanish in exactly the multi-corps Italian battles it exists for — pinned). No morale regen exists anywhere, so he still eventually breaks or dies — no unroutable pincushion.

**Kutuzov — the engineered duel.** His pursuit-halving fires against exactly one marshal in 1805 (pursuit damage is ability-only in code; no base pursuit exists) — **the dependency is on Murat's First Horseman of Europe landing**, and the ordering (halve AFTER the +5k → net 2,500) gets a pin test. Rejected from the package: survivor-band doubling (erodes the encirclement counterplay) and zero-attrition. Encirclement shatter, W6-7 fate, and morale grind are all untouched — three lethal lines remain. **Stacking rule for the gate:** if logistics-based attrition resistance is ever wired, his combined retreat-attrition reduction is capped at ×0.5 (ability supersedes; no multiplicative stacking with his logistics 8).

**Moore — the designated first cut.** The 10–12 band is a ceiling, not a quota; if you want 9 abilities, cut this row. It survives adversarial review on its merits (single-site seam, GR5-automatic, airtight 1805 history — Shorncliffe is literally what he was doing), and it's the only ability whose primary beneficiary is the AI — correct GR5 behavior, blessed explicitly (Q6). The rejected variant (scouting) was killed on feasibility: the enemy AI never scouts and is omniscient by design. The recruit-as-morale-heal edge (rebuilding a shattered army toward 60) is bounded, expensive, and thematically the point.

## 5. MC-4 recommendation — personality coverage + the Soult question

**Recommendation: Path (b) — do NOT implement `balanced`/`loyal`.** Re-author differentiation entirely within the three existing types using what this pass delivers. All three design lenses independently reached this conclusion. The reasoning, compressed: a new personality type is a CR-5-magnitude platform slice (two V2 evaluator functions, objection templates, defiance fallback rows, five strategic-doctrine forks, a user-gated delegation arm, corpus/eval re-validation, a voice bank, ~15 copy surfaces — realistically 2–4 full sessions per type), while a half-shipped type inherits an incoherent chimera that, worst of all, **objects to nothing** — silently diluting literal's user-blessed never-objects fantasy. And the player never feels the 13-cautious skew: player-facing France is a healthy 4 aggressive / 2 cautious / 1 literal; the cautious pile-up sits in enemy majors where it is correct casting. The two "missing" personalities are expressible cheaper in this very pass — Bernadotte's political unreliability is trust 40 + three negative edges + the Eyes on a Crown ability; "loyal" is high trust + the relationship web.

Per Golden Rule 9, the deferral closes as a contract, not an open question: strike the "unimplemented placeholders" framing; the **only** re-open owners are the Jealousy v3.1 gate (v3.2 roster addendum) and the MC-3 exit review, on evidence that three types are expressively insufficient AFTER content lands. If a fourth type is ever revived, the on-ramp is the AI-side personality-remap precedent, and it must not be named "loyal" (namespace collision with the diplomat `loyalist` type — several files read `getattr(x,'personality')` on both object kinds). Recommended hardening rider (one line, not a slice): log-or-assert on any non-three-type personality at scenario boot, so a modder typo cannot ship the chimera.

**Soult: KEEP literal — and canonize it as character.** The 1805 frame genuinely rehabilitates the CR-5 recast: this is the one campaign where Soult operates under Napoleon's direct hand, and there he was the supreme executor — he drilled the Boulogne camps to clockwork and delivered the Pratzen assault at the exact appointed hour. His independent-command flaws and "King Nicolas" ambition belong to Spain 1809+, outside this scenario. Concretely: (i) MC-1's Drillmaster of Boulogne makes literal-Soult read as the machine-precise first maneuverer of Europe; (ii) MC-2 adds the one-line bio touch-up ("executes the Emperor's plan to the letter, exactly on the appointed hour"); (iii) his ambition lands in trust 70 + the Soult–Ney −1 edge. Then **retire the "one disclosed exception" language** in `MARSHAL_CONTENT_PASS_SPEC.md` §1 and `COMMAND_ROBUSTNESS_SPEC.md` §6.8 — after canonization the personality=character rule holds with zero exceptions, which is strictly stronger. Rejected alternatives: recasting Davout literal (his cautious identity is load-bearing for Iron Resolve and for the man — the reverted-Massena mistake again) and adding an 8th French marshal (no 1805 candidate is a literal archetype; the roster is scenario-authored and balance-coupled). **Cross-dependency, stated plainly: if the gate cuts Drillmaster of Boulogne, this canonization loses its centerpiece — decide the two together.** The MC-3 exit review owns the live-play re-check; `test_cr5_literal_arm_player_reachable` stays the pin.

**Cost:** near-zero beyond work MC-1/2/3 already own, plus ~30 minutes of spec/doc surgery (MC-4 row closed with the named re-open trigger, §6.8 exception retired, `ADDING_CONTENT.md:94-95` note updated, STATUS line).

## 6. Build sequencing

MC-0 is done (landed July 4). Proposed order, honoring the land-a-small-slice-then-pause review cadence:

1. **MC-1a — T0 quick wins (first, small, review checkpoint).** Ney re-key (with the load-bearing `"trigger": "when_attacking"` key) + Charles +3% activation + all ten `_WIRED_ABILITY_MARSHALS` entries. Pure JSON/data + display; backend restart (lru_cached registry). Pin tests: ability name on boot, `ability_triggered` on attack / silent on defense, card shows active.
2. **MC-1b — the T1 set (the bulk).** Soult, Lannes, Murat, Massena, Bernadotte, Kutuzov, Moore, Charles rout-threshold. Test plan: one pin test per ability at its seam; **both pursuit copies exercised** for Murat AND Kutuzov (the two-copy silent-gap trap); the Murat-vs-Kutuzov ordering test (halve after +5k → 2,500); Lannes/Bernadotte arrival deltas + SUPPORT interaction + honest muster-reason arms (W6-4 — the preview must never lie); Charles coordinated-path rout isolation; Massena 1.75-cap stack test; GR5 symmetry tests (enemy-initiated battle adjacent to Lannes; AI recruit path picks up Shorncliffe). Every ability carries its two mandatory riders: battle-report row or Berthier observation, and campaign-log one-liner where it creates an event.
3. **MC-1c — Davout T2 Iron Resolve (last, alone).** New `iron_resolve_stacks` serialized field → `to_dict`/`from_dict`/`SAVE_FORMAT_REFERENCE.md` + `test_serialization_enforcement.py`; pin the stacks-survive-unfortify rule and the clear-on-move/retreat rules; battle-report stack row (snapshots run pre-consumption — verified safe).
4. **MC-2 — skills + trust.** Author per §3 (with the Q3 admin/command decision applied); per-marshal pin tests; **re-measure and record** Ney's ability at shock 9 and the Lannes/Bernadotte arrival numbers at their authored logistics.
5. **MC-3 — relationships.** The 13-pair web + symmetric-pair post-load pass + pin tests; hand the graph to the Jealousy v3.2 addendum.
6. **MC-4 — doc surgery only** (per §5) + the one-line boot guard rider.
7. **MC-V — personality-kit assurance + opposing-AI personality evaluation (USER-ADDED RIDER, July 10, 2026 — part of the package, not an open question).** Rationale: this memo wires 10 signature abilities, but for the other 11 marshals the personality grants ARE their entire mechanical identity — and 9 of the 10 enemy-side marshals have no ability at all. Assurance that the personality layer exists and is *played* is therefore load-bearing for the whole pass. Two halves, both landing inside this pass (owner row: `MARSHAL_CONTENT_PASS_SPEC.md` gains an MC-V row at blessing; STATUS tracking line at landing):
   - **(a) Assure the personality-type kit exists and fires for every marshal, BOTH sides.** Pin each type's grants per side through the SAME executor/combat path (Golden Rule 5): aggressive (+15% attack, recklessness escalation) asserted for a player marshal (Ney/Lannes) AND an enemy-controlled aggressive marshal in an AI-initiated battle; cautious (counter-punch free attack, defensive-stance/outnumbered grants, fortify rate) for e.g. Davout AND Archduke Charles/Kutuzov defending on the AI turn; literal (1-AP strategic orders, executes exactly, never-objects doctrine) for Soult AND enemy Mack/Buxhowden.
