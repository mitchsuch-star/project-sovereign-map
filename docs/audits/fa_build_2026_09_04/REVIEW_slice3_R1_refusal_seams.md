## Findings

**1. P1 - The aggressive cannon-fire REDIRECT is a twelfth order-driven attack seam, it is not read by the predicate, and it destroys the order on a refusal.**
Seam: `backend/commands/strategic.py::StrategicOrderProcessor._handle_interrupt` (the `"redirect"` arm - fires at end turn from `_check_interrupts` for every aggressive marshal within 2 provinces of a recorded battle; no player word is involved). It sets `marshal.strategic_order = None` BEFORE `executor.execute(attack)`, never consults `attack_was_refused`, and returns `order_status: "interrupted"`, `action_taken: "attack"`, `action_success: False` with the message `"{name} hears cannon fire! Abandoning orders - rushing to {X}! {refusal}"`. The client renders `message` only.
Repro: `p1_redirect.py` -> `p1_redirect.out.txt`. (a) Ney at Normandy, Moore at London, a battle recorded at London: row = *"Ney hears cannon fire! Abandoning orders - rushing to London! The crossing from Normandy to London is barred - the Royal Navy commands the water ..."*, `strategic_order` None, location unchanged, no battle, no casualties. (b) A land refusal reachable without a fleet: Mack co-located with Ney at Rhineland, a battle at Nassau with Moore in it: *"... rushing to Nassau! Cannot attack elsewhere while engaged with enemy forces! Mack must be dealt with first."*, order gone, nothing moved. (c) AST: `_handle_interrupt`, `_handle_move_to_arrival`, `_respond_cannon_fire` each carry an attack literal and none calls `attack_was_refused`.
The record classifies this arm among "five answer/redirect arms the player's own word drives" - it is not; it is the FA-20 shape inverted (the refusal DESTROYS a live order instead of leaving a phantom one). The `investigate` answer arm (`_respond_cannon_fire`) has the same shape for the player's word: *"{name} abandons his march and charges into battle at {X}! {refusal}"*, `order_cleared: True` (see `p8_reckless_redirect.out.txt`, second half).
Fix: in both cannon-fire arms snapshot the order, run the attack, and on `attack_was_refused` RESTORE it and say why (the WO-28 idiom `jealousy.process_autonomous_attacks` already uses: order, hold state, order-bound interrupt), returning `action: "attack_refused"` with `refusal_keys`; never clear the order before the executor has answered.

Repro: `p3b_stale_range_march.py` -> `p3b_stale_range_march.out.txt`. Davout at Rhineland with a pending contact on Mack "at Nassau"; Mack now at Flanders (distance 2); AP 3.
