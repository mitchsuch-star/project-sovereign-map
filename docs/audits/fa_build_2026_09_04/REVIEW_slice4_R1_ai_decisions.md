P4 guards the same helper with `max(_defenders, int(enemy.strength))` (`enemy_ai.py:3014`); the two sites this slice added do not. This is the counter-punch's founding scenario: the player's attacker is repulsed, forced back one province beside a friend, and the flag survives into the enemy phase (cleared only at `world_state.py:9175` in `advance_turn`; `probe_cp_e2e.out.txt` shows `retreated_this_turn=True` on Ney when Britain's phase runs).
Repro `probes\probe_cp_exclusion.py` -> `probe_cp_exclusion.out.txt`: Wellington 20k (cautious, banked blow) at Belgium; Ney 30k `retreated_this_turn` + Davout 5k at Paris. Field helper = 5,000 -> P3.25 orders `attack Ney` (20k/5k = 4.0 over a floor ~1.25); with the flag off the same board is declined at 0.57. Executed: Wellington 20,000 -> 17,560, Davout -174, Ney untouched ("Davout steps forward to cover Ney's retreat"). Range arm, same shape two hops out: `[STAGNATION] Uxbridge: Attacking Ney with lowered threshold 1.10 (ratio 4.80)`.
Seam: `enemy_ai.py` `_get_counter_punch_action` (the `field = ... or enemy.strength` line) and `_get_stagnation_action` range arm (same line). Fix: `field = max(field, int(enemy.strength))` at both, matching P4.
