## 4. Attempted and NOT reproducible

- **A misroute or client popup from the nested `state`/`redemption_event` on an enemy-phase action** — no reader exists on either side (grep + `p3`: HTTP 200, `jsonable` True, top level clean).
- **A nested `new_state` reaching `jsonable_encoder` from any producer other than the rider's** — the charge's `combat_result` is the resolver's output; every other whole-result embedding strips at the producer or the boundary (table above).
- **Any AI decision or combat number moved by a player latch** — `p1`: Bernadotte latched from t16 with `series == BASELINE_SERIES`, autonomous/administrative empty every turn; `p6`: `to_dict` byte-identical around an AI-marshal check.
- **Any write before the nation guard** — `standing_redemption` (the only pre-latch write) is 0 calls for an AI marshal even with a stale world question present.
- **A double question when both men are LIVE** — `p4` Variant C: Davout (15) asked, Lannes (10) not latched; Lannes asked once Davout was answered. The one-live rule holds whenever the first question is live; it fails only for a non-live first man (R2-2).
