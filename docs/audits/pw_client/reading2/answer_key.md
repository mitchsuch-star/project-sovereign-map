# Row PW — the SECOND blind reading: the bar and the answer key (written BEFORE the readers read)

**Why a second reading.** The first (three fresh Sonnet readers, the frames of the tree before these fixes)
met five questions of six with every reader and MISSED question 6's first part with all three: none could
say what "Offer them more" would do before pressing it ("the screen does not say"). The lever built from it
(PW-X13): a dial wears what its next press does. The frames below are the final tree's, re-shot at the first
player's own setup; the battle's numbers differ from the first key because the campaign's seed differs.

**The bar (unchanged):** three fresh readers who have never seen the game are shown only the frames A, B, D,
E, F and asked the same six questions. The bar is met only if EVERY reader answers EVERY question correctly,
by the key below. A reader's "I cannot tell" is a miss.

| # | Question | Correct means |
|---|---|---|

Dig in: costs 1 point, +21% defence if he attacks (helps only if he attacks); he moves next. Wait: free, he moves next and will attack. Retreat: free, the battle ends and he takes the province. |
