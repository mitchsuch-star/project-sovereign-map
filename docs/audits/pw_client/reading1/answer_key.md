# Row PW — the blind reading: the bar and the answer key (written BEFORE the readers read)

**The bar:** three fresh readers who have never seen the game are shown only the frames A, B, D, E, F (the
real client at the first player's own setup) and asked six questions. The bar is met only if EVERY reader
answers EVERY question correctly, by the key below. A reader's "I cannot tell" is a miss.

**Frames:** A the war card on the map · B the peace table after one press of "Offer them more" · D the
Cabinet's page for an enemy · E the report after End Turn · F the battle that has no winner yet.

| # | Question | Correct means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | (F) In one line each: what happens if you press Counter-attack, Dig in, Wait, Retreat? | Counter-attack: attack now, costs 1 point, about 10–11 thousand men against about 49 thousand, odds bad / will probably lose. Dig in: costs 1 point, +21% defence if he attacks (helps only if he attacks); he moves next. Wait: free, he moves next and will attack. Retreat: free, the battle ends and he takes the province. |
| 2 | (F) Who is doing better in this battle so far, and how can you tell? | The enemy (Austria / Archduke Charles): France has lost far more men (about 6,000 against about 2,000); "he has lost fewer"; there is no winner yet. |
| 3 | (B) If this deal were signed exactly as shown, what would you get and what would you give? | Get nothing; give 100 gold to each of Austria, Britain and Russia (300 in all). |
| 4 | (B) Will they sign it as it stands? Who refuses, and why? | No — "They will not sign yet": all three (3 of 3: Austria, Britain, Russia) refuse; the stated reason is that the war has decided nothing yet. |
| 5 | (A, D) You want to end this war. What can you press? | The "Make peace" button on the war card; or, in the Cabinet under END THE WAR, one of: Make peace — choose the terms / Make peace with no terms / Offer peace to Britain alone / Ask for a truce. (Either door named correctly is a pass.) |
| 6 | (B, E) What will "Offer them more" do, and what does "Leave out" on Russia's row do? In frame E, what happened to Swabia and why? | "Offer them more": offers the courts more gold (the deal changes; it did: 100 gold each was added). "Leave out": leaves Russia out of this peace — it stays at war. Swabia (Bavaria's, an ally's) was lost to Austria: Mack marched in. |
