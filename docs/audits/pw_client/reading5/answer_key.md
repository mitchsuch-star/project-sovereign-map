# Row PW part 3 — the blind reading's bar and key (November 19, 2026)

**The bar, written before any frame was shot:** three fresh readers (Sonnet) who have never seen the game are
shown four frames of the real client at the first player's own setup (Interface Scale 1.8 on 5120 x 1377) and
asked six questions; the bar is met only if EVERY reader answers EVERY question correctly by this key. A
reader's "I cannot tell" on what the key asks is a miss. The frames: **C** a general selected on the map (the
command desk, his card, the top bar, the map's controls) · **K** the map's key, open · **D** the Cabinet's
page for an enemy · **O** a marshal's objection.

| # | Question | The key |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | (C) On the left, what do "Actions", "Admin" and "Recruits" with their numbers tell you? | Actions: how many orders (actions) you have left this turn, of how many; Admin: the same for administrative actions; Recruits: the men you can still recruit, in three kinds (foot / horse / guns — or infantry / cavalry / artillery). |
| 2 | (C) On the general's card, what does "2 Fortify · 2 actions" mean — what is the first 2, and what is the second? | The first 2 is the key (or the slot's number) that gives the order; the second is its price: Fortify costs 2 actions. |
| 3 | (C) Name the four figures at the top right of the screen and say what each is. | Gold (the treasury and its change a turn), Diplomacy (diplomatic points), Authority, Threat. |
| 4 | (K) On the map, what is: a round token with "?"; a dark box reading "12k"; a ship with a number in a circle; a red anchor? | "?" — a force seen through the fog, its size not known; "12k" — a garrison of 12,000 men holding the province; the ship — a fleet and its number of ships; the red anchor — a port under blockade. |
| 5 | (D) Would this court accept the peace named on the page today? How far short is it, and what weighs most against it? | No. The plain line gives the figure it stands at and what they agree at (50), and how far short; the weight named in the plain line (and the largest minus in the table). The exact figures are the frame's own (written below once the frame is shot, before any reader sees it). |
| 6 | (O) Whose is the "trust" figure and whose is the "authority" figure? If you press "By My Word", what happens to each? | Trust is the marshal's (his trust in you); authority is yours (the player's). By My Word: he obeys, no trust is lost, and it costs you authority (the figure on the button). |
