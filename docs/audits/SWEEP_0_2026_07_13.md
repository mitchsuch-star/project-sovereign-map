### What each baseline confirms (maps to spec §3 root causes)

- **M1 = flat, M1b = 0/0/0 — concentration is mechanically inert.** With `COMMITTED_ALPHA = 0.0` (the faithful model of `combat.py:1018` resolving on the **lead** marshal only), adding corps 1→5 changes the win-rate by exactly 0.000, and a reinforcer's contribution to committed strength is identical (zero) regardless of personality or relationship. This is the keystone flaw: massing corps — the core Napoleonic verb — does nothing. A lone 40k corps cannot crack a dug-in 40k mountain-fort defender (the Mack-in-Swabia scenario), so the curve sits at the floor; CO-1's additive strength lifts it monotonically (α·Σ contribution reaches ~200k committed at 5 corps).
- **M2 = 0.000 / 0.000 — defenders are near-unbreakable.** Even a maintained 3:1 superior force routs a defender within two turns essentially never: casualty-scaled morale loss is small against `FORCED_RETREAT_THRESHOLD = 25`.
- **M3 = +4251 — frontal attrition is unwinnable.** A sustained 2:1 assault removes ~5,749 men/turn from a dug-in defender (measured from real resolution), but the uncapped enemy per-corps regen (`AI_CORPS_REGEN_PER_TURN = 10000`, the Field-Review Mack `+10,000/turn` figure) more than replaces them, so the defender **gains** ground under attack.
- **M4 = 0.0% — the survivor two-truths bug.** In every multi-marshal battle the report derives `attacker_remaining` from the lead-vs-lead casualties (`battle_report.py:865` → original − casualties), while the downstream event uses the lead's strength *after* the total casualties are distributed across participants (`combat_executor.py:3874`). Verified: the coordinated path only reconciles the report for pursuit damage (`combat_executor.py:4000`), never for the attacker's remaining, so the two contradict.
- **M5 = +0.116 — Iron Resolve is stance-trapped.** Stacks only accrue while fortified, forcing the DEFENSIVE stance (−10% attack); releasing three stacks (+24% raw) from that trapped stance nets only +11.6% over a plain attack (`0.90 × 1.24 = 1.116`), well under the +18% the ability should pay. (The spec's §2.1 "+4%" was a looser field estimate; the code-measured figure is +11.6% — both below the +18% target. CO-7 frees the stance → ~+24%.)
- **M6 = 0.000 — the defender edge is intact (the guard).** A solo equal-strength assault into fort+mountains never wins across 400 seeds. This is the anti-over-nerf sentinel: the decisiveness work (CO-3) must **not** trip this above 0.5 in any later sweep.
- **M7 = never — the drama engine is dormant (the triple lock, spec §3.2).** Over an 8-turn frontal-attrition run at authority 100, no France marshal ever develops a grievance: stalemate-dominated combat records ~0 glory (`jealousy.py:154`), the 5-turn `GLORY_WINDOW` decays what little forms, and `authority > 70` adds +1 to every threshold. All three break in Phase 3.

### Two modelled knobs (the only forward-looking constants in the harness)

Both live at their **baseline** value in `tests/test_combat_sweep_metrics.py` and are read by the metrics, so the future phase flips one constant + its paired assertion:

- `COMMITTED_ALPHA = 0.0` — CO-1 (Phase 1) sets the sweep-tuned α (start 0.6). Flips M1 (flat → monotonic) and M1b (0/0/0 → aggressive > cautious > hostile).
- `AI_CORPS_REGEN_PER_TURN = 10000` — CO-4 (Phase 2) replaces this with the capped code path (R, start 3,000, exempt in a friendly depot/capital). Flips M3 (net ≥0 → net ≤ −2000).

The combat halves of every metric (casualties, morale, rout, report, iron modifier) are measured from the **real** resolution; only these two not-yet-landed fixes are represented as constants, as the Phase-0 contract requires.

### Modelling notes (for the future session)

- **M2/M3 "sustained assault"** refreshes the attacker to the stated ratio each turn (a *maintained* superior force) while the defender's morale/strength carry — isolating numerical decisiveness / attrition from attacker fatigue. Ratios are **raw** strength (2:1, 3:1); the defender's flat +20% bonus makes the *effective* ratio lower, which is why the baselines sit near zero.
- **M7** loads the 1805 scenario once, keeps the France roster engaged each turn (so the literal-sidelining path is not spuriously tripped), feeds glory through the real `record_battle_glory`, and calls the real `jealousy.process_turn` — a faithful reproduction of the reviewed play pattern, not a stub.
