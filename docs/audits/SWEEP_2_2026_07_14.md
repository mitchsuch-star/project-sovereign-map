parsing), cross-checked against the twelve component JSON reviews and the raw evidence log (read
directly to confirm the headline beats). Scores carry the usual ±0.5 LLM jitter — material for the

unexercised pillars (Diplomacy / Settlement / Vassals are flat-by-decoupling, not flat-by-
observation); the two unproven drama arms need a differently-driven session.
