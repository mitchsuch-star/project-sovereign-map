## The method
A blind author — an agent that never opened the corpus, the tests or the docs, given only the roster, the map and the verb families — wrote **150 orders** (each with its intended reading) and **150 questions** (each with the answer it needs): `tools/playtest_scripts/unrehearsed_2026_10_03.json`, never edited after the run. `tools/unrehearsed_census.py` runs each line on a FRESH 1805 boot board through the real `POST /command` and reads every reply with the ONE judge the HOLD arm uses (`tools/_score_probes.py`: `ACTION_WORDS`, `BOARD_GATE_RX`, `ASKED_RX`, `REFUSED_RX`, `SHRUG_RX`), keyless and keyed. The two records are committed under `docs/audits/unrehearsed/` and are re-read by the shipping judge in the pins, so a judge that changes re-reads the evidence.
