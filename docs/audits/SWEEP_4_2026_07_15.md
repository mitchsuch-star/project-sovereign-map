### Headline — did Vassals hit ≥6.5? **YES — 6.5, target MET (at the floor).**

Earned and honest, not rounded. The July-14 playtest scored the pillar exactly **6.0** and named
the cause precisely: VS-1 surfaced the invisible −2 bleed (good) but taught **broken levers** to fix
it — a "garrison their capital" reading unassigned `region.garrison_troops` (contributes 0), a "large
subsidy" with no command, a "grant autonomy" typed dead-end. A system *lying* to the player about
its own options. All of it was fixed in `016bf71`, and VS-R added the real content: a derived grip
that finally reflects military collapse when the raw tracker can't, with a coherent
"no-cheap-recovery" spiral. The **+0.5 is the correct modest number** — the pillar removed its own
lies and added one bounded, collapse-only mechanic; it did not yet deepen into the coalition-dynamics
beat its name promises.

**What holds it short of 7 (all CONFIRMED, none regressions):**
- **VP-D1** — the garrison-in-capital lever is still inert dead code (`vassal.py:326-341` +
  `main.py:3589`), correctly dropped from the hint but left in wire-or-remove limbo.
- **Coalition-defection is unbuilt** — a satellite can only rebel to independence, never switch to
  the enemy coalition (the marquee "Coalition Dynamics" beat; deferred by design as a GR9 slice).
- **VS-R is structurally a punishment-for-losing amplifier** — from healthy authority ~100 the grip
  floor is 45, never below the 30 spiral threshold; the spiral requires a pre-depressed authority
  AND a lost capital, so a healthy player never experiences the layer.
- The marquee **drama economy** (crown/petitions/estates) still needs a winning run to exercise.
