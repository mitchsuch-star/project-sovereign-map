## 2. The blocking gates, measured

| Court (design) | Peak weight while OPENABLE | Dominant blocks (turns, across seeds) |
|---|---|---|
| Prussia (`hanoverian_prize`, acquire vs Hanover) | **59–63, price `align`** — never within reach of 85 while at peace | `price<align>` every seed until Austria overruns Prussia (see §5) |

| Sweden / Sardinia (vs France) | 72–86 | `price<coerce/bandwagon>` + player target beyond |

Three structural readings:

1. **The at-peace acquire ceiling is exactly §0.2's.** Prussia — the
   marquee AI-vs-AI case — never left `align` while openable, on any
   seed. Base 55 + chilly 4 = 59; jitter peaks add ≤ 8. The 85 bar is
   unreachable without the §2.2 terms.
2. **The type filter has a real, measured constituency.** Britain and
   Russia sat AT the fight rung for 10–15 turns per seed and were
   blocked by nothing but `want_type != "acquire_regions"` — §2.3's
   widening is repairing an observed block, not a hypothetical. (On this
   scenario their `against` then resolves to France-the-player while
   France is hegemon, so the v1 player-exclusion takes over — a
   deny/contain COUNCIL war needs a non-player-hegemon geometry. That is
   the D3 guard working, and §5-4's "written blocking predicate" arm.)
3. **The restraint gates were never the binding constraint** for the
   observed zeros: every anti-France court fails even the RAW 1.25×
   force ratio against France's 189k (rawOK=False at every high-water
   mark) — the price/type filters block long before force does. The
   exposure gate (.1) therefore tightens a gate that today's zeros never
   even reached; it exists for the wars .2 makes possible.

## 4. Unexpected finding: the un-counselled war channel

On the historical ambient run, **Prussia declared war on Austria at turn
5** — no crisis, no fore-warning, no `ai_initiated` flag — via the
pre-existing **combat-seam auto-declaration** (`combat_executor.py`
~3501: an AI marshal's attack on a peace-nation declares war before the
battle, the Phase-8 seam that pin 15 gates on a *successful*
declaration). The enemy-AI action layer can therefore already open an
AI-vs-AI war as a target-of-opportunity, outside the ladder and the
cause taxonomy.

**Disposition:** OUT of AI-3r's mandate (this spec amends D1 only; the
seam is Stage-D-accepted and pin-15-gated). Recorded here because (a)
the .V sweep must not credit these wars to the council — the
`ai_initiated` flag is the discriminator, and (b) AI-V's acceptance arm
should observe both channels. No spec change; no code change.

## 5. Ambient-world caveat for reading the .V sweep

Idle-France ambient worlds diverge fast from the live game: with no
player holding the coalition, Austria overruns Bavaria and then Prussia
(survival override by turn ~5–6 on every seed), Britain's Moore takes
Flanders/Amsterdam by turn 4, and the boot wars end by AI-4b settlements
mid-run. The live pin-20 pass (human France, 38 turns) showed the same
zeros with Prussia sitting quietly at `align 59` — the two worlds agree
on the DEFECT even though their dynamics differ. The .V acceptance
numbers are measured on the ambient harness and recorded as measured
anchors (the E1-anchor precedent), with the live in-game check (§5-7)
as the human-eyes confirmation.
