# Round 1 ledger — findings and rulings (pitch v2)

## Panel 2 — Designers (Voss, Brodie, Morita, Adeyemi)
Scores: A6 B7 C8 D4 E6 F5 G7.

| # | Rank | Finding | Ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 1 | "Serve the plan": the object is free to change, so it re-tasks every serving commander for nothing (a Change costs 1). | ACCEPT — changing the object costs 1 action while any man serves it; they turn that night. |
| D2 | 1 | Literal men sit outside the cap: more literal men = more free actors; "if enemy in reach, attack" is a command-lite. | ACCEPT — orders to the letter get their own cap, equal to the command cap. Each line still fires once. |
| D3 | 1 | "March to the guns" lets a commander fight in the enemy's phase and then act again at night. | ACCEPT — joining a battle spends that night's act. |
| D4 | 1 | Fog symmetry not stated: whose sight do enemy commanders use; does a hunter steer by a hidden army's true position? | ACCEPT — every commander decides on what his own nation can see; a hunter steers by the last sighting. |
| D5 | 2 | Trust farm: +5 for three quiet nights on a Watch, stand down, repeat. | ACCEPT — reward only for a task done or a battle fought, once per marshal per ten turns, lost if stood down. |
| D6 | 2 | The road to his ground is undefined. | ACCEPT — an explicit "on the road" state: ordinary lawful march, arrow shown, he does not seek battle; a barred road stops him and he reports. |
| D7 | 2 | The one step out: a baited man cut off cannot be "back next night"; does a battle won out there take the province? | ACCEPT — he returns by the nearest lawful road and says so; a battle won out there takes nothing. |
| D8 | 2 | Scope: too many parts for one build. | ACCEPT — restage (see the build plan). |
| D9 | 2 | Dispatches on every win + spies revealing all = information becomes routine. | ACCEPT — a dispatch only when the beaten corps routs or breaks; the spy reports marshals inside that nation's land only. |
| D10 | 2 | Recall pump: feint at the capital, court recalls and re-gives, bleeding its actions. | ACCEPT — recall only on a real threat (today's recall rule); a recalled command is not re-given for five turns. |
| D11 | 3 | Free daily adjust makes him a puppet. | ACCEPT (voice only) — after three adjusts in five turns he says so. |
| D12 | 3 | Hold's free dig-in must not exceed the usual fortify cap. | ANSWERED — it is the ordinary fortify, with its cap. |

Edge cases accepted: the Wing dissolves before the cap check when the Staff lapses · change + overrule the same day: overrule today, new ground tomorrow · read-back's two buttons each show their own price · trust under 30: he steps out on every chance · hunted army unseen: quiet act, one standing row · a Wing follower ordered by hand leaves the Wing for the day · the junior of two attackers re-decides if the enemy retreats · a captured commander earns no reward · "then hold" removed from the list (standing is his default) · a Take that is done becomes a Hold of that place at once · the Emperor arriving makes it his battle and the marshal's letter complains · a petition with no place free can be promised for two turns.
Enemy: a one-corps nation takes no commands · measure that enemy tempo does not fall.
