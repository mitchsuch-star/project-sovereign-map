## THE BIG RULING OF ROUND 2 — the enemy's side is cut down
Two rounds scored the enemy's side lowest (E: 4 to 6), for the same reasons: enemy corps are not short of actions; pulling them out of the court's hand into "roles" breaks existing behaviours (recapture, threat response, stagnation), changes tempo, needs successor and assignment rules, and needs per-nation fog that does not exist. What both rounds PRAISED was narrower: literal marshals under written orders from seeded authored sets; public natures; learning orders by spy, dispatch and Berthier.
Ruling: enemy ROLES and the "capital in danger" rule are CUT. The enemy keeps its hand exactly as today. Each court draws a PLAN OF CAMPAIGN from authored plans by the seed; a plan writes its literal marshals' exact orders and sets two dials the court's logic already reads (its posture, and one province its corps favour).

## Other cuts of round 2
- Attached corps (the Wing): cut. A second man set to Support the first does the same job; "a higher man supporting a lower one takes it ill" is the existing rule.
- The one-night lag on adjustments: cut. One price for any change.
- Counting literal men: cut. The count limits judgement only.
