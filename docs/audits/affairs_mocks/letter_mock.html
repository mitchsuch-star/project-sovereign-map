An Affair of State — Late March 1806
N
The Squadron for Santo Domingo — Decrès, Minister of Marine and the Colonies, lays a letter before the Emperor.

Sire, Ferrand wrote last February that he had bread for twenty days and no credit; Your Majesty called it shameful to abandon these brave men. Brest can put five of the line to sea in a month.

Historically the law of 20 May 1802 kept slavery in the colonies Amiens returned, and acts of 1802 restored it in Guadeloupe and Guiana; the expedition sent to break Toussaint Louverture's rule ended in Haiti's independence, 1 January 1804. Haiti killed the French who stayed in 1804; Ferrand's decree of 6 January 1805 let his raiders sell the Haitians they took, and Dessalines marched on Santo Domingo, killing civilians at Moca and Santiago on his retreat.

From Haiti —"Slavery is forever abolished." — the Constitution of Haiti, 20 May 1805, Article 2: three years to the day after the law.
  • France blockaded 9 of the last 12 mornings; the Emperor holds court at Paris.
  • Britain's squadrons hold the coast: 100 sail effective against our 22.
