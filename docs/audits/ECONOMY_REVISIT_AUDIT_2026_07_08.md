## Executive Summary

The 1805 economy has a rich, historically literate *transfer* layer (indemnities, subsidies, tribute, treaty gold, plunder, settlement payments — all reconciling to Net through the landed PRE-EC ledger floor), but the *dynamics* invert the two facts that should drive play: there are sources with no proportional sinks, and neither currency is actually scarce. France nets roughly +3,792 g/turn with gold effectively free (treasury 800→28k in eight turns), upkeep is flat-linear at `(strength//1000)*5` covering only ~28% of income with no force limit, and manpower — meant to be the binding brake — refills itself (cavalry ~+12,250/turn against a 30k cap; artillery's "urban" regen bonus is dead code keyed to a `terrain` value no province has). Conquest yields full income the turn after capture with zero occupation cost, so the economy actively feeds the snowball instead of braking it. Most damning against VISION: the treasury has **zero interface to the marshal-loyalty emotional core** — `economy_executor.py` contains no trust or relationship reference at all, so "territory has faces, not numbers" is entirely absent from the money layer. The fix is not more spreadsheet: it is (1) make manpower scarce, (2) add super-linear/occupation drains proportional to empire size, and (3) add one sink the player *wants* that couples gold to a marshal.

**E2 — ES-1 blessed regen/cap numbers (EC-3).** Every constant is a blessed tuning number requiring sign-off: `PLAINS_CAVALRY_REGEN` 500→~150, capped plains bonus; artillery re-key rate 200→~80 with cap ~600 (NOT a straight 200 re-key — that's a fresh runaway); and the proposed *scaling* of `MAX_CAVALRY_POOL=30000` / `MAX_ARTILLERY_POOL=20000` with nation size. *Option A:* land the exact proposed values. *Option B:* land the re-key bugfix + caps only this slice, retune rates in a follow-up. **Recommendation:** Option A with the two-sided band test as the acceptance gate — but the numbers are yours to set.

**E3 — ES-3 super-linear upkeep constants (EC-3).** Base rate 5→~8–9 and the force-limit coefficient *k* are blessed. **Recommendation:** set them to hit the E1 band, and budget the effort as M+ (the surcharge must reconcile across all 7 upkeep callers + ledger + dispatch).

**E4 — EC-5 member self-cost (A.4 Q3).** *Option A:* income-bite only (Britain −40g/member; no French self-cost — safest, but omits the defining Berlin-Decree self-harm). *Option B:* symmetric self-cost (~−30g/coastal-French-region) for the authentic "the embargo hurts you too" tension. **Recommendation:** Option B, gated on the two-sided solvency test (a symmetric bite can zero a minor member AI). If the AI-solvency test is red, fall back to Option A for pass 1 and own the self-cost as a named follow-up.

**E5 — ES-7 marshal-economy coupling sign-off (A.4 Q5).** ES-7's trust step touches territory the un-approved Marshal Content Pass + Jealousy specs also claim. *Option A:* hold ALL marshal-economy coupling until those gates clear (ES-2 ships alone as a pure tax — loses the whole play-feel win). *Option B:* land the minimal `modify_trust`-only seam now (explicitly out of `modify_relationship`/Jealousy), with its own owner row + test, that those specs later extend. **Recommendation:** Option B — it unblocks the highest-impact win without pre-empting the un-approved specs, provided the seam stays on `modify_trust` and the loss-penalty fires AI-side.

## Top Three Bets

1. **ES-1 manpower fix (gate-free, first).** Without a scarce second currency, *no* gold sink creates a real decision — a rich player just re-recruits. This is the cheap, load-bearing prerequisite that makes everything downstream matter. Split the re-key bugfix from the retune; escalate the numbers.

2. **ES-2 Occupation Upkeep + ES-7 Dotations as one matched slice (EC-2 gate).** Together they convert conquest from free profit into "digest before you bite" AND give the flush treasury the *only* sink that couples gold to a named marshal — closing the single biggest gap against VISION. Neither works alone. Ships with the AI branch, the reconciling ledger lines, and the minimal `modify_trust` seam.

3. **The GR5 AI-admin branch + two-sided band test, as named deliverables.** The most likely way pass 1 fails is shipping a sink the AI never uses (de-facto player-only) or a drain that bankrupts a minor AI into a passive death spiral in a no-lose sandbox. Making the AI heuristic branch and the France+AI solvency band test *deliverables* — not afterthoughts — is what keeps the whole pass honest.
