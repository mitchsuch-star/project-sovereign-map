## 6. The evidence, corrected before the readers marked

Each of these was found by checking the evidence itself, fixed in the TOOLS (never the client), and the affected surfaces re-taken; the records say so.
- **The main menu was shot mid-animation.** Its entrance (the title over 1.1 s, the buttons staggered from 0.5 s) runs in real time, and off-screen frames are not time. Both menu shots were re-shot with a 2.5-s real-time settle (`settle_ms`) and merged; that removed 15 false contrast rows.
- **The probe's fresh-instance lookup pressed the wrong button.** Auto-named siblings (`@Button@`) share a normalised path, and the lookup pressed the first of them for all of them; the wizard's nation rows read SILENT. Fixed; all 142 surfaces were re-probed.
- **A setting one press wrote leaked into the next instance.** The war room's Scale + changed the in-memory scale, and later clicks landed beside their controls; at first this looked like the Scale − button being covered. The probe now restores the settings and the scale before every open; the six affected surfaces were re-probed.
- **"Replay" was over-matched** by the probe's never-press words. It replays the diorama's own tableau; the five dioramas were re-probed with it pressed.
- **The host rule.** A HUD piece shot alone (the top bar, the war HUD, the rail, the tutor card) cannot answer without the war room. Where the war room's own probe pressed the same control and it answered, that record stands for it, and the packet showed both. It flipped the top bar's six nav buttons and its Menu (and the Admiralty chip) on the three top-bar boards, the war HUD's rows on its four boards, and the tutor card's "✎" link.
- **The title screen is shot with no backend**, as every IQ-10 menu shot is. Its campaign buttons wait disabled for a connection a real launch makes; R5's reader ✗ on them (no reason shown) stands as read.
- **Reader 2** disclosed that it read `records/probe.json` with shell tools and a Python one-liner (read-only) where its brief said not to run programs. It opened no other reader's work and wrote nothing outside its own directory; its marks stand.
