## What landed (single-source constants)

- **CO-3 decisiveness → capture.** New module-level helper
  `combat.decisiveness_morale_penalty(loser_casualties, winner_casualties)` —
  extra morale loss for the side losing a lopsided casualty EXCHANGE (out-bled
  ÷ other), keyed on the ratio not absolute size (so an equal-strength defender
  who trades evenly — M6 — is untouched). Wired into BOTH morale paths (the
  immediate `resolve_battle` and the deferred coordinated `_calculate_casualties`)
  on the out-bled side of every decisive/tactical outcome. Symmetric (GR5).
  Blessed constants (sweep-tuned): `DECISIVENESS_EXCHANGE_PIVOT = 1.75`,
  `DECISIVENESS_MORALE_SLOPE = 22`, `DECISIVENESS_MORALE_CAP = 55`. Does **not**
  lower `defender_bonus` (0.2 flat, M6 intact).
- **CO-4 cap enemy regeneration.** `economy_executor.AI_CORPS_REGEN_CAP = 3000`
  + helper `region_has_friendly_supply(region)` (capital or supply-depot →
  exempt). A corps reinforcing in the field (no depot/capital) recruits a capped
  levy: `_execute_recruit` honours `command["reinforcement_cap"]`; the enemy AI
  recruit rungs (P1 urgent + P7 rebuild) supply it when the corps is forward.
  GR5: shared recruit executor, the supply context is the differing input; the
  player's base recruit at a capital/depot is untouched (satisfies the exemption)
  and retreat-recovery is a separate path. The harness knob
  `AI_CORPS_REGEN_PER_TURN` mirrors the production cap (guarded by
  `test_regen_cap_matches_production`).
- **CO-6 reinforcement legibility.** A coordinated battle with arrived,
  contributing reinforcers now prints the committed effective strength —
  `Massed effective strength: <lead> (lead) + <committed> committed (<names>) =
  <total>.` — so the CO-1 additive model is visible. A rival reinforcer (CO-1b
  ×0.0) correctly prints no line (it contributed nothing).
- **CO-7 Iron Resolve stance fix.** Releasing the coil is no longer self-
  cancelled by the fortify-mandated DEFENSIVE stance: `marshal.get_attack_modifier`
  undoes exactly the ×0.90 base defensive-stance attack penalty when Iron Resolve
  stacks fire (personality mods untouched, read-only when `consume=False`). The
  3-stack release now lands its full +24% (M5 +0.240).
