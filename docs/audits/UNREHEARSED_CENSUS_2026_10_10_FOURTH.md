## The method
A fourth blind author — an agent given only what a player sees on the 1805 boot screen (the roster and where each corps stands, the four enemy commanders in sight and their courts, our provinces, the provinces next to our corps, the courts and who we are at war with, the treasury, the turn) and the ONE judge's family names for `intended.action` — wrote **300 orders**, no questions, in deliberately varied registers, with **30 lines meant to be refused**. The transcript shows **zero tool uses**: the author never opened the repository, the corpus, the tests, the docs or the three earlier blind files. The JSON came back in one message and was saved as written. Each line ran on a FRESH 1805 boot board through the real `POST /command`, keyless (`LLM_MODE=mock`) and keyed (`anthropic`), read by the ONE judge (`tools/_score_probes.py`, as it stands after S4 part 1), every row carrying its parse trace.

## What the measurement says
The Reading closed the ledger and the third set's shrugs because those were the classes the instrument had already named. A FRESH author names new ones at once — above all the march register's idioms and the telegraph's variants — and the keyless number falls from 59 % (rehearsed) to 40 % (fresh). The ≥ 85 % done-when will not be reached by rewrite rules alone; it needs the spine split's second half — the readers consuming the Reading's slots (addressee, verb, objects) instead of re-scanning a composed string — so that *"Murat — rhineland — go."*, *"ney franconia pls"* and *"Marshal Davout is to march his corps to Franconia"* are the same reading.
