# Player notes - the diplomacy road (1805 campaign, reached Turn 21)

**What I tried.** I went straight to the Cabinet (F1) and read Talleyrand's page for each court at war with me. On turn 1 every offer read "Hopeless" (war score 0), so I sent Talleyrand to warm Prussia and took the Open Borders letters that Prussia, the Ottomans, Portugal, Saxony and the Papal States sent me (each added a little trade income). The Cabinet's own numbers told me what the table wanted: a won battle. I fought one limited campaign on turn 2: one order, "Ney, attack Mack", pulled in five corps and the Emperor, and I destroyed Mack twice over. That moved Austria from "Hopeless" to an armistice offer I could buy for 115 gold a turn. Austria's truce became a peace on turn 8 by itself, because I put Talleyrand on Improve Relations during the truce. I then repeated the pattern with Russia (a 283 gold a turn armistice plus Improve Relations, peace on turn 14) and with Britain (Talleyrand's "Seek terms" counsel produced a no-indemnity white peace offer on turn 10, which dissolved the Third Coalition, threat 78 -> 37). I used my 10-20k idle gold on "Buy Off Their Design" for Sweden, Austria, Britain and Russia, a free Licence to Prussia against Hanover (it answered with a Non-Aggression Pact and 150 gold), a Licence to Russia and to Austria against their own targets, Code Abroad to steady the satellites, and Increase Autonomy / Crown Louis for Switzerland and Holland.

**How far I got.** All three wars with the Third Coalition ended at the table (Austria turn 8, Britain turn 10, Russia turn 14). I played to Turn 21 with no enemy corps in view and a threat level of 45/100. I did not proclaim an Emperor's Design (all three are irreversible and Road I was blocked once Bavaria fell) and I did not reach the Congress of Paris (35 of 45 titled provinces). I lost Bavaria (an ally Austria overran while my truce left her to fight alone) and my Spanish alliance decayed to a Defensive Alliance with no lever to stop it.

**The three things that confused me most.**
1. **Numbers that disagreed or were hidden at the moment of choice.** The Cabinet said "Propose Armistice - 44 of 50" for Britain and the preview of the same proposal said 24; an armistice preview said "war score reads 0" and "nothing taken yet" on the page that also said "War score: 38"; the preview's last line was "(1 more concern...)" with no way to open it. Talleyrand's page said a payment of 975 gold "would carry the table" but the only gold control is per turn, so I could not find how to pay a lump sum.
2. **Spain.** Spain fell from +33 to +12 in two turns after I ratified the British peace (a "Grievance Modifier (-30)" with no cause named), then the alliance collapsed to a Defensive Alliance. The Cabinet's Spain page had no Improve Relations and no explanation, so I could only watch.
3. **Interface moves with no confirm or no explanation.** "Buy Off Their Design (1368g)" spent the gold at once with the explanatory text clipped; the Open Borders envoys had no explanation of what open borders means; and "Improve Relations (+8/turn)" with Prussia read "+0 over 2 turns" for several turns without saying what was cancelling it. Also, the Envoys panel's lettered rows only opened when I clicked the row itself (the "Open" word is not a button).

**Smaller things:** one-diplomat limit made me cancel and re-start missions often (every switch was 3-4 clicks, and the Recall button on the notice rail only showed when the rail was expanded); the Cabinet's list resets its scroll each time so reaching Britain/Russia took a scroll plus a click every time; the Emperor's Designs page is a long wall of text with the key irreversible choice buried in it.
