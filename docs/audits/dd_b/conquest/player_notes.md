# Player notes — the conquest road (1805 campaign, Turns 1–20)

**What I tried.** I began the 1805 campaign from the title screen. I massed the corps against Mack in Swabia and destroyed him by the end of turn 1. I broke Archduke Charles in Franconia and Tyrol (he was taken prisoner), walked into an empty Bohemia, and took Vienna on turn 4 by three garrison assaults. Austria then signed a separate white peace that left Bohemia, Tyrol and Vienna titled to me. Russia sat on Austrian soil in Moravia, where the peace now barred my army, so I took a truce. Britain offered a white peace on turn 9 and I signed it. After that the Congress of Paris (45 titled provinces) became the goal. I declared war on Prussia on turn 10 and took Silesia, Brandenburg, Pomerania and Posen. I later raided Lithuania. Each conquest raised Europe's alarm, and the Fourth Coalition formed on turn 15 with eleven courts at war with me. The Russian truce had already lapsed.

**How far I got.** I reached Turn 21 (July 1806) with the campaign still running. I won the first war outright, with Vienna taken and three Austrian provinces titled by treaty. The second half went badly. Morale across the army sank to 0–30%. Lannes, Ney, Soult, Massena and Murat were all captured. The Russians retook Vienna. I finished with 35 of 45 titled provinces, so the Congress was out of reach. Most of the disasters were my own over-reach. But several came from orders that did something other than I expected.

**The three things that confused me most.**
1. **What an order will actually do before it fires.** The muster appears in the same reply as the battle result, so it reports rather than previews. "Attack Kutuzov in Moravia" silently became a 2-AP pursue that walked Ney alone into Saxony. An attack into Moravia went in with one corps because a peace treaty had made that soil off-limits to the rest, and nothing warned me. The Glorious Charge sent Murat in without the muster that every other attack had brought. Supporting corps relocate into the battle province, which emptied Bohemia twice. Fortify silently cost 2 AP. "Recruit artillery for Augereau" recruited infantry. The last action of a turn ended the turn by itself.
2. **Losing marshals without being told how or why.** The "capture looms: Fight to the Last / Attempt a Breakout" card gives no odds. For Lannes, Ney and Murat no result line appeared at the time. "Where is Ney" answered "I cannot answer that from the dispatches". Only `status` (or next turn's prisoner list) told me they were prisoners. Davout twice objected "the enemy is too strong" against a 375-man post and an "undefended" Vienna, and overruling him cost trust.
3. **The peace and diplomacy screens contradicting themselves.** "Counter" on an envoy sent terms I never chose and lost the offer. "Press Austria" in the settlement window means *demand more*, though the advice beside it uses "Press" for a separate peace. "More generous" lowered Prussia's willingness, and for Saxony it swapped the armistice for an open-ended 500 g/turn. Peace texts said "no map redrawn" next to "Vienna stays ours". The Balance of Europe told me to send Talleyrand to Saxony, but no such button existed. The Imperial Box rail kept stale cards for many turns ("Treasury in deficit", "Coalition Brewing — 3 turns").
