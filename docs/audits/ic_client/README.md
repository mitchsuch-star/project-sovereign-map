# Row IC "Independent Command", the desk — the blind readings (November 19, 2026)

The frames are the real client at the first player's own setup (Interface Scale 1.8 on a 5120 x 1377
window: a logical view of 2844 x 765), shot by the instrument (`tools/dd_b_play.py`,
`DD_B_UI_SCALE=1.8 DD_B_WINDOW=5120x1377`): **D** the main menu on a machine with no saved campaign and no
finished lesson · **A** the first morning of the 1805 campaign · **C** End Turn pressed once, with matters
standing · **K** the map's key.

**The bar, written before any frame was shown to a reader:** three fresh readers (Sonnet) who have never
seen the game are shown only the frames and asked the questions; the bar is met only if EVERY reader
answers EVERY question correctly by the key (each reading's `answer_key.md`, written from the frames before
the readers were asked). A reader's "I cannot tell" on what the key asks is a miss. A miss is reported as a
miss; a lever built from it gets another reading with fresh readers.

**The result, in one line:** three readings, nine fresh readers, **54 answers, 54 right by the key** — and,
more useful than the score, each reading's readers were asked what confused them, and what they said was
built before the next one.

## Reading 1 — `reading1/` — MET (21 of 21)

Every reader quoted the card for the aim ("hold 45 titled provinces, then summon the Congress of Paris — you
hold 35"), named all three matters when End Turn stopped, said "Press End Turn again, or Enter" and "press one
to go to it", and read the red triangle from the key and matched it to "Nothing holds Rhineland".

**What the readers said confused them** (asked of each; none is a miss by the key):

| Said by | What | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| all three | The menu has TWO lit rows — "Begin" keeps its gold face beside the School's glow: "not clear which one is the default". | **Lever L1, built:** while the steer stands, "Begin" wears the plain face; one row is lit. |
| all three | The "!" in front of a matter is not explained. | **Lever L2, built:** "! can cost you when the turn ends" — a line under the card's rows and in the list's head. |
| reader 2 | "Rhineland" is named in the list, but the red triangle on the map has no name. | **Lever L3, built:** the mark wears its province's name on a small plate (and the map's own names step clear of the plate — seen in the real client: it stood over "HESSE"). |
| reader 3 | "Actions 0/4" — a matter such as "6 corps have no orders" cannot be acted on. | **Lever L4, built:** with no action left, the corps with no orders are no matter on the card and no reason for End Turn to stop. |
| all three | The last reply in the log is cut off under the list ("He will drop this order for…"). | Ruled "by design" here; the second reading's readers took the cut-off line for one more matter, so it became **lever L6**. |
| all three | Words of surfaces this row did not touch: "Actions" / "Admin" with no explanation on the face, the top bar's Authority / Threat / Diplomacy, "45 sail · BLOCKADED", the war card's slider, the Emperor's Designs paragraph, "Lorraine · arriving" badges standing on a name plate. | Not this row's surfaces. Each has its hover (row PW part 3) — a frame cannot hover. Carried to the deploy session's reading as notes, not as defect rows. |

## Reading 3 — `reading3/` — MET (6 of 6)

Shot after levers L5 and L6. Two questions, each sharpened to ask for exactly what reading 2's readers could
not tell: **A** "How is this game won, and how far along are you? And what is a 'titled province' — which
provinces count as one?" · **B** "How many matters does it list, and what is each? Is anything else on that
part of the screen part of the list?" All three readers named the three kinds of titled province from the
line, and all three said two matters and nothing else.

**What the readers said confused them, and what was done** — the last four were built after this reading
and were NOT put to a fourth one; each was pressed in the real client at the same setup and is pinned by the
driven tests (`tests/test_dd_b_the_client_hands.py::TestRowICTheDesk`):

| Said by | What | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| readers 2, 3 (and reader 1 of reading 2) | "Is it one list shown twice, or two lists?" — the card's TO DECIDE and the box above End Turn. | **Built:** the list's head now uses the card's own words — "Still to decide before the turn ends — 2 matters (…)". It is one list; the words say so. |
| readers 1, 2 | "Ney will leave his standing order" — "a standing order that I never gave? Is joining the battle good or bad?" | **Built:** the row names what he breaks off in the order's own plain word — "Ney will break off his march to join a battle near him — Mack is close." |
| readers 1, 2 | "Actions are 0/4 … I am told to decide, but I have no actions left." | **Built:** with no action left, the list's last line says the one remedy still in hand — "No actions are left — Take back (Ctrl+Z) undoes your last order." (only while the game offers it). |
| reader 1 | "The box has a big empty gap at the top." | **Built:** the list's rows stand in the middle of its panel, not at its foot. |
| all three | Which provinces on the map are the 35; how the Congress is summoned; whether the 12 turns must be unbroken. | Answered one press away, not on the line: pressing THE PEACE opens the Congress page, which lists every condition and which are met. A frame cannot press. The map does not mark titled provinces; that would be a map view of its own, and is not promised here. |
| all three | "! can cost you" does not say WHAT it costs. | Each row says its own consequence (a province an enemy can walk into; an order broken off). Kept. |
| all three | "×", "–", "one march", "corps", "(large force)" with no number. | The game's words and marks; each has its hover, and "(large force)" is all the fog allows of a half-seen army. Notes for the deploy session's reading. |

final build differs from `reading3/` by the four changes in the last table; `final/` holds the same two
