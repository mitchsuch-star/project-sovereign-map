## §1 Why (measured, October 23, 2026, on the booted 1805 world and the shipped client)

- **The Cabinet** (F1; `diplomacy_wizard.gd:110`): title "DIPLOMACY", "DP: 5", the prompt "Your Excellency, which nation requires our diplomatic attention?", then the rows. No line names what a court can be asked, what DP is or that it returns; "DP" is never spelled out on steps 1–2; the screen is titled DIPLOMACY while every pointer in the game calls it the Cabinet (S14D-X9, X10). `diplomacy_wizard.gd` is absent from the R159 pin.
- **The Emperor's Designs** (the road page, `_render_designs` :675): "Three roads, Sire — what the Empire will be. One proclamation decides it, and it cannot be undone." — not what a road is made of (a construction, then a seal), what it costs (2 DP, 1 administrative action) or what it earns (the Decisive Peace).
- **The settlement table** (`proposal_confirm_popup.gd:560-643`): on turn 1 it mounts for 0 DP and 0 AP (`open_settlement` available on the Austria page) and reads "This settlement cannot be ratified now … Holding out: Austria −8/50, Britain −8/50, Russia −8/50 … sit at 0 because France has not fought them." Nothing names the table, the per-court gate or the three levers; the heading says "ratified" on a PROPOSE table that has no Ratify (S14D-X6). A fighting France (Mack destroyed on turn 1, +32 live) never carries Austria alone by turn 5 (39, 42, 35, 38); only a truce carries (45, 47, 51 on turns 2–4).
- **The separate peace**: a chip on a court's row, drawn only at or above the threshold (`settlement_staging.py:3934-3944`) — never on turn 1 — then the chooser "Leave the joint settlement? … Treat with X alone — every other court keeps its war." The ally consequence is hover-only on the chip; the chooser's dialogue carries no `separate_peace_words` (S14D-X7). The same act has five names (S14D-X34).
- **The LAWS book** (`_render_laws_tab` :1132): header, dateline, six rows — the only book with no R159 subtitle; the two currencies appear per row unexplained; the lapse and the Arrears show only on a lapsed row. DD-B's diplomacy player never opened it (DDB-D7): the only pointers are plain text, no chip (S14D-X3).
- **The Admiralty's camp** (`_render_admiralty_block`): "The Camp: No army is staged: 0 of the 40,000 men a descent requires stand in the camp (Flanders, Artois, Normandy, Brittany) — it stages after 2 turns there at war with Britain, no order needed." — the count and the rule, never what staging DOES (Britain recalls her fleet, the blockade on France lifts) or what can open the Strait; the warning line above it uses "staging" before the row defines it (S14D-X25). "Descent" is never shown to the player.
- **The muster's free preview**: `what_if_line` rides every resolved muster and the W6-4 hint once; the gate road — the `muster_confirm` interrupt and its Cancel — never names the preview, and that road is the first muster a cautious or outmatched player meets (S14D-X4). - **The moment letter**: "A MOMENT OF HISTORY — {dateline}", the record, the board, the two quotes, the unanswered line with a date, Not Now — nothing says what a moment is or that it waits two turns (the S14c hand-off, `EVENTS_SPEC.md` §11).
- **The five engine hints** are the precedent: once per campaign, latched on the surface, serialized. ## §10 Risks, re-open conditions, siblings

- **The fold.** A line at the head of the table, the chooser, the letter, the road page or the Cabinet can push a button below the fold at Interface Scale 2.0 — the IQ-10 `must_show` pins at both scales are part 1's gate, not an afterthought (SFR-B11, EA-E6); a line that fails them is shortened, never moved below the fold.
- **A line burned unseen.** The acknowledgement fires on draw, and every line is drawn at the head so that drawn = visible; the frames pin it. A lost acknowledgement (the client closes before the POST lands) shows the line again next time. Those are the two directions the design can fail in, and both are harmless.
- **The board drifts** with a balance change elsewhere (a scorer term, a relation rule, the AI's letter triggers): the arithmetic pins of part 2 red the drift at its commit, which is what they are for; the margin is 3 points.
- **The enemy phase** (FA-63): an authored quiet front is a measurement; the pin holds it for the script's three turns and the board moves Charles if it does not.
- **The release build** ships without the fourth file: S14D-X15 makes the pin name every school scenario, so it cannot.
