### 0.6.2 ES-7 (reframed) — gate-ready mechanic: "The Cost of Success"

> **One line:** A marshal who wins builds a rising expectation of reward; you meet it by **endowing him with an estate and title in a conquered province** (its income becomes his — a real recurring skim); an unmet or revoked expectation erodes his loyalty. **Success is an obligation, not a purchase — paying only stops the bleed, it never buys trust.** This is the falsifiable heart of the reframe: *no payment ever raises trust on demand.* (Supersedes the Appendix-A ES-7 "+15 trust on grant" bribe text entirely.)
>
> **⚠ AMENDED at the July-9 gate (§0.6.7, authoritative):** the endowment is a **FULL-income redirect** — satisfaction = the estate's full `eff_income`, the nation loses the same, the 0.30 skim constant is DELETED (the 30%-skim arithmetic below could never meet the 300 cap — a permanently-bitter-veteran trap). Also blessed: grace-turn **2**, grant scope = **conquered (non-homeland) provinces only**, estate provinces **exempt from the ES-2 occupation cost**. Read §0.6.2 below through those four amendments.

> **Realism framing (user steer, July 8 cont. — RESOLVED, §0.6.6).** The mechanic is a recurring income redirect, but it is presented as **"endow Marshal Ney with the Duchy of {province}"**, NOT as an abstract "grant a dotation." This is the single most historically faithful reward on the table — it is literally how Napoleon paid his marshalate (the *Domaine Extraordinaire*: Marmont's 50,000 fr/yr from Dalmatia; Davout *Prince of Eckmühl*; Ney *Prince of the Moskva*). The player *experiences* it as "I honored my general with an estate and a title"; the income-skim is the estate's fiscal consequence (his revenue funds his dynasty, not your army); losing the province strips a prince of his lands. The **honorific title is derived at grant-time from the province name** ("Duke of Swabia"), flavor-only (Golden Rule 6), so it needs **no ES-8 per-region-victory greenfield** — the endowment already knows the province. This folds ES-8's *title fantasy* into pass 1 as free flavor; the ES-8 *mechanic* (a per-(marshal,region) victory source + a pinned stat/trust bonus) stays a deferred Appendix candidate.

**Expectation model (deterministic, GR6).** `expectation(m) = min(REP_STEP × m.battles_won, EXPECTATION_CAP)` — a per-turn income figure the marshal feels owed, derived from the **existing flat `battles_won` counter** (dodges the ES-8 per-region-victory greenfield). Expressed in the same gold/turn units as a dotation's yield, so expectation and satisfaction are directly comparable — *that comparability IS the legibility.* Blessed: `REP_STEP ≈ 40 g/turn/win`, `EXPECTATION_CAP ≈ 300 g/turn` (~one province tier).

**Reward action (player-facing = "Endow with an Estate").** `grant_dotation` (internal action id) — a new admin action (full 12-step new-action wiring), surfaced to the player as **"Endow {marshal} with the Duchy of {province}"**: tags a **player-held, non-capital, non-vassal, un-dotated** province to a marshal, conferring a province-derived honorific title (flavor-only). Cost = **1 AP + ~200g one-time investiture fee**. Effect: each turn, **30%** of that province's `get_effective_income()` (which automatically reflects ES-2's occupation ramp) is redirected to the marshal's estate. **NO trust bump on grant** — the endowment is a promise, not a purchase (the reframe's core). `satisfaction(m) = Σ 0.30 × eff_income(r)` over his still-held estate regions. Surface: a Marshal-Management card `[Endow ▸]` affordance + a province picker scoped to co-located/adjacent regions ("reward the man where he fought"); typed `endow <marshal> with <region>` / `grant <region> to <marshal>` is debug-tier. Re-endowing is the recovery path (1 AP, no fee).

**Erosion curve.** Per-turn marshal reconciliation in ONE place (`_process_dotation_state`, §0.6.1 #6). `shortfall = max(0, expectation − satisfaction)`. `erosion = -min(EROSION_MAX, ceil(shortfall / SHORTFALL_PER_POINT))` applied via `modify_trust`. Blessed: `EROSION_MAX = -3/turn`, `SHORTFALL_PER_POINT ≈ 50g`. **Grace-turn debounce:** erosion fires only if the shortfall was ALSO present last turn (serialized `expectation_grace_turn`) — kills the "I won a battle and instantly lost trust" un-fun beat. **Clamp:** trust's native floor at 0 is the *only* floor; erosion magnitude never grows with the gap (self-limiting, no snowball). The −3/turn cap gives ~10 turns of visible warning before the objection band — always a recovery path. **No reputation-scaled trust floor** (rejected — redundant tuning surface, and a hard floor would undercut the "a stiffed marshal can eventually desert" tension).

**Solvency-trap bind (state-driven, seam-independent).** Because satisfaction is recomputed each turn from *currently-held* dotation regions, ANY way a funding province leaves the player's hands — a peace cede, a recapture, a rebellion, a vassal grab — drops satisfaction the next turn with **no seam-specific hook.** Cede Ney's province in a peace deal → next turn satisfaction→0, shortfall→full expectation, erosion→−3, card flips red, dispatch fires (Lens-1 copy): *"The province that funded Marshal Ney's honor has been bargained away. He is bitter."* The peace that saves the empire starves the man who won it — a gold decision with a face, felt because the game showed the expectation first.

**Serialized state** (on `Marshal`, in `to_dict` AND `from_dict` with `.get()` defaults; `test_serialization_enforcement.py` gate): `dotation_regions: List[str] = []` and `expectation_grace_turn: int = -1`. **Reputation needs NO new field** (derived from `battles_won`). No new `WorldState` field (dotations live on marshals). Constants live in a new `backend/game_logic/dotation.py` (file discipline mirroring `vassal.py`). **Save-compat (R7):** `from_dict` sets `expectation_grace_turn = current_turn` when the field is absent AND `battles_won > 0`, so old saves don't take an instant retroactive erosion on load.

### A.0 Diagnosis (why the economy is flat)

The economy has **sources but no proportional sinks, and neither currency is scarce.** France nets **+3,792 g/turn** and balloons 800 → 28,228 g in 8 turns because the only recurring drain — army upkeep at a flat `(strength//1000)×5` — covers just **28%** of income and scales strictly linearly, so a snowballing army never self-taxes. Conquest adds full net income the turn after capture with **zero digestion cost**, so winning makes you richer with no friction (the classic runaway-leader failure). The second currency meant to be the brake — **manpower** — is also running away (cavalry `+12,250/turn`, dead urban-artillery regen), so a rich player just re-recruits. Net effect: **gold has no opportunity cost**, so there are no interesting decisions, and the marshal-loyalty core has no economic interface at all.

**Ordering principle (do not reorder):** ① make manpower scarce again → ② add recurring drains that scale super-linearly with empire/army size → ③ give the flush player a sink they *want* (marshal rewards) so drains read as ambition, not punishment.
